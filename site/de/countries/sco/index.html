Alle Details (Englisch)
  • SCOTTISH PREMIERSHIP

    12 clubs · triple round robin (33 matchdays) · after 33 matchdays: 1-6 single round robin, 7-12 single round robin · home order: the club that hosted the other fewer times · 38 matchdays · 1 down

    • PREMIERSHIP PLAY-OFF promotion play-off: SCOTTISH CHAMPIONSHIP 2nd-4th + SCOTTISH PREMIERSHIP 11th; 3 rounds of two legs, home order as in the real schedule, extra time and penalties; the winner to SCOTTISH PREMIERSHIP
  • SCOTTISH CHAMPIONSHIP

    10 clubs · quadruple round robin, 36 matchdays · 1 up · 1 down

    • CHAMPIONSHIP PLAY-OFF promotion play-off: SCOTTISH LEAGUE ONE 2nd-4th + SCOTTISH CHAMPIONSHIP 9th; round 1: two legs, home order as in the real schedule, extra time and penalties; round 2: two legs, the better placed at home (2nd leg), extra time and penalties; the winner to SCOTTISH CHAMPIONSHIP
  • SCOTTISH LEAGUE ONE

    10 clubs · quadruple round robin, 36 matchdays · 1 up · 1 down

    • LEAGUE ONE PLAY-OFF promotion play-off: SCOTTISH LEAGUE TWO 2nd-4th + SCOTTISH LEAGUE ONE 9th; round 1: two legs, home order as in the real schedule, extra time and penalties; round 2: two legs, the better placed at home (2nd leg), extra time and penalties; the winner to SCOTTISH LEAGUE ONE
  • SCOTTISH LEAGUE TWO

    10 clubs · quadruple round robin, 36 matchdays · 1 up · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

  • SCOTTISH CUPCup

    32 clubs · from the round of 32 · the clubs still in the real 2026-27 cup, 1 of the 16 first-round pairs as really drawn · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties · rounds numbered as in reality

  • SCOTTISH LEAGUE CUPLeague cup

    8 clubs · from the quarter-finals · the clubs still in the real 2026-27 cup, all 4 first-round pairs as really drawn · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties · rounds numbered as in reality

  • Europe

    season 1: the real 2026-27 league-phase clubs (Europa League Celtic; Conference League Hearts) · from season 2: Europa League 1, Conference League 1 by the simulated association coefficients
