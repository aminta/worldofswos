    • EUROPEAN PLAY-OFFS European play-off: CYMRU PREMIER 2nd-7th; 3 rounds of single match, the better placed at home, extra time and penalties

    16 clubs · from the round of 16 · the clubs still in the real 2026-27 cup, 2 of the 8 first-round pairs as really drawn · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties · rounds numbered as in reality

    16 clubs · every club of the league, drawn by the game · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties
