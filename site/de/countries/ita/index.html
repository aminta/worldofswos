Alle Details (Englisch)
  • SERIE A

    20 clubs · double round robin, 38 matchdays · 3 down

  • SERIE B

    20 clubs · double round robin, 38 matchdays · 2 up · 3 down

  • SERIE C GIRONE A / SERIE C GIRONE B / SERIE C GIRONE C

    3 parallel groups of 20 + 20 + 20 (each club in the group of its area; promoted and relegated clubs join the group of their area) · double round robin, 38 matchdays · 1 up · 1 down

    • SERIE C A PLAY-OUT play-out: SERIE C GIRONE A 16th-19th; two legs, the better placed at home (2nd leg), level: the better placed goes through, not played when the two clubs are 8+ points apart
    • SERIE C B PLAY-OUT play-out: SERIE C GIRONE B 16th-19th; two legs, the better placed at home (2nd leg), level: the better placed goes through, not played when the two clubs are 8+ points apart
    • SERIE C C PLAY-OUT play-out: SERIE C GIRONE C 16th-19th; two legs, the better placed at home (2nd leg), level: the better placed goes through, not played when the two clubs are 8+ points apart
  • SERIE D GIRONE A / SERIE D GIRONE B / SERIE D GIRONE C / SERIE D GIRONE D / SERIE D GIRONE E / SERIE D GIRONE F / SERIE D GIRONE G / SERIE D GIRONE H / SERIE D GIRONE I

    9 parallel groups of 17 + 17 + 17 + 18 + 17 + 18 + 17 + 17 + 18 (each club in the group of its area; promoted and relegated clubs join the group of their area) · double round robin, 34 matchdays · one club rests each matchday · 1 up · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

    • SERIE D A PLAY-OFF placement play-off: SERIE D GIRONE A 2nd-5th; 2 rounds of single match, the better placed at home, extra time, then the better placed
    • SERIE D B PLAY-OFF placement play-off: SERIE D GIRONE B 2nd-5th; 2 rounds of single match, the better placed at home, extra time, then the better placed
    • SERIE D C PLAY-OFF placement play-off: SERIE D GIRONE C 2nd-5th; 2 rounds of single match, the better placed at home, extra time, then the better placed
    • SERIE D D PLAY-OFF placement play-off: SERIE D GIRONE D 2nd-5th; 2 rounds of single match, the better placed at home, extra time, then the better placed
    • SERIE D E PLAY-OFF placement play-off: SERIE D GIRONE E 2nd-5th; 2 rounds of single match, the better placed at home, extra time, then the better placed
    • SERIE D F PLAY-OFF placement play-off: SERIE D GIRONE F 2nd-5th; 2 rounds of single match, the better placed at home, extra time, then the better placed
    • SERIE D G PLAY-OFF placement play-off: SERIE D GIRONE G 2nd-5th; 2 rounds of single match, the better placed at home, extra time, then the better placed
    • SERIE D H PLAY-OFF placement play-off: SERIE D GIRONE H 2nd-5th; 2 rounds of single match, the better placed at home, extra time, then the better placed
    • SERIE D I PLAY-OFF placement play-off: SERIE D GIRONE I 2nd-5th; 2 rounds of single match, the better placed at home, extra time, then the better placed
    • Serie B play-offs (3rd-8th: preliminary 5v8, 6v7 at the better placed; two-legged semi-finals and final; level: the better placed) and play-out (16th v 17th, two legs); Serie C play-off of 28 clubs (group phase, national phase, Final Four) for the fourth promotion
  • COPPA ITALIACup

    16 clubs · from the round of 16 · the clubs still in the real 2026-27 cup, all 8 first-round pairs as really drawn · rounds 1-2: single matches, extra time and penalties · round 3: two legs (aggregate, then extra time and penalties) · final: single match, extra time and penalties

  • COPPA ITALIA SERIE CLeague cup

    17 clubs · from the round of 32 · the clubs still in the real 2026-27 cup, the only first-round pair as really drawn · 15 byes into the 2nd round · rounds 1-3: single matches, extra time and penalties · round 4: two legs (aggregate, then extra time and penalties) · final: two legs (aggregate, then extra time and penalties)

  • Europe

    season 1: the real 2026-27 league-phase clubs (Champions League Como, Inter, Roma, Napoli; Europa League Juventus, Milan; Conference League Atalanta) · from season 2: Champions League 5, Europa League 2, Conference League 1 by the simulated association coefficients

  • Declared approximation

    the 9 Serie D clubs that come up at the end of season 1 are the Serie D group leaders of 18 October 2026, standing in for the real winners (known in May 2027)
