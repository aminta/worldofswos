Alle Details (Englisch)
  • SUPER LEAGUE GREECE

    14 clubs · double round robin (26 matchdays) · after 26 matchdays: 1-4 double round robin, 5-8 double round robin, points halved (rounded up), 9-14 double round robin · 36 matchdays · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

  • GREEK CUPCup

    11 clubs · from the round of 16 · the clubs still in the real 2026-27 cup, 0 of the 3 first-round pairs as really drawn · 5 byes into the 2nd round · rounds 1-2: single matches, extra time and penalties · round 3: two legs (aggregate, then extra time and penalties) · final: single match, extra time and penalties

  • Europe

    season 1: the real 2026-27 league-phase clubs (Champions League AEK Athens; Europa League OFI Crete, Olympiacos; Conference League Panathinaikos) · from season 2: Champions League 1, Europa League 2, Conference League 1 by the simulated association coefficients
