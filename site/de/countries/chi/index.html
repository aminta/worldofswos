Alle Details (Englisch)
  • LIGA DE PRIMERA

    16 clubs · double round robin, 30 matchdays · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

  • COPA CHILECup

    10 clubs · from the round of 16 · the clubs still in the real 2026 cup, all 2 first-round pairs as really drawn · 6 byes into the 2nd round · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties

  • COPA DE LA LIGALeague cup

    16 clubs · every club of the league, drawn by the game · rounds 1-2: single matches, extra time and penalties · round 3: two legs (aggregate, then extra time and penalties) · final: single match, extra time and penalties
