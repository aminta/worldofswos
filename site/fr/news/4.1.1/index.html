Les nouveautés de la 4.1.1

Télécharger le patcher

4.1.1 is a small update that fixes a few things: in the 2026-27 world, New Zealand's championship final, the group draws of the continental cups and the Recopa / Intercontinental matches of a career club still in a cup outside Europe, a line on the Coppa Italia Serie D page and the Serie D Poule Scudetto winner sent to the Champions League; in every world, a cursor that could get stuck on a screen's title; and, in the patcher, the joypad boxes and reading the GOG game folder in Chrome. Everything in 4.1 stays as it was; careers saved with 4.0 up to 4.1 load and play on.

Nouveautés

New Zealand: the championship final is played by the two real finalists

When your club was not in the New Zealand National League final, the game still played a match for the title between your club and another club of the group, as if your club were there: your club could pick up a card or an injury in a final it never played. Now the final is played by the two clubs that really reached it, and the champion is the result of that match.

Continental cups: fairer group draws

Continental cup group draws no longer put two clubs from the same country in one group, except a club that came through the qualifying rounds, where the competition's own rules allow it. Copa Libertadores and Copa Sudamericana pots now follow the real CONMEBOL club ranking (ranked clubs first; unranked clubs by squad strength, as before).

Coppa Italia Serie D: the FORMAT page tells the whole rule

The FORMAT page of the Coppa Italia Serie D now says that the home and away alternation (the club that was away in its previous tie plays at home) only goes up to the quarter-finals. The semi-finals and the final are played over two legs.

Fixes

  • Fixed: on some screens (for example the COPPA ITALIA SERIE C results) the cursor could get stuck on the title bar and could not move down.
  • Fixed: winning the Serie D Poule Scudetto no longer puts the club in the next season's Champions League; the European places come only from Serie A and the Coppa Italia.
  • Fixed: in the 2026-27 world, a career club playing a continental cup's group stage outside Europe (Copa Libertadores, Copa Sudamericana, CAF Champions League, AFC Champions League Two) never got to play its later Recopa Sudamericana or Intercontinental matches that season, and at season end the cup's winner could disagree with what happened on the pitch (your club named champion after being knocked out). Now those matches are played right after your club goes out of the cup; if it reaches the final, they are decided at the season end, and the cup's winner is the club that won the final.
  • Patcher: ticking "GOG version, joypad in keyboard mode" now also ticks "GOG version: I have a joypad", and unticking the first box unticks the keyboard mode too. Playing with the keyboard only? Leave the joypad boxes unticked.
  • Patcher in Chrome: Chrome does not let a web page open folders inside Program Files, where the GOG version installs, so the first button could not read the game there. The second button now reads the game folder in Chrome too (Chrome asks you to confirm the upload; your files stay on your PC); or use Edge.

Bon à savoir

  • Careers saved with 4.0, 4.0.1, 4.0.2, 4.0.5 and 4.1 load and play on. In an older save, a continental cup that has already started keeps its draw; the new rules apply from the next edition.
  • You do not need to start a new career to get the New Zealand fix: it applies from the next Grand Final that is still to be decided (a save made after the Championship group ended and before its final keeps the old behaviour for that one final).
  • The Poule Scudetto fix also needs no new career: it applies from the next end of season. A save made after a season has ended keeps the European places already given out for that season.
  • A career saved with 4.1 in the middle of a season picks the continental-cup fix (Recopa / Intercontinental) up at its next match; a cup winner already named at an earlier season end stays as it was.
