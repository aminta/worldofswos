What's new in 4.0.2

Download the patcher

4.0.2 puts two things first: the game now always runs in full graphics, and the real kits of 227 Italian clubs. It also brings bigger squads and buying players from other clubs' extra players, a CONTROLS menu inside the game, editable shirt numbers, a keyboard mode for joypads on GOG, more leagues in their real format (Guatemala, El Salvador, Paraguay), fixes to the European places and a long list of fixes to menus and screens. Everything in 4.0 and 4.0.1 stays as it was; careers saved with 4.0 or 4.0.1 load and play on (see "Good to know").

What's new

In the spotlight

  • Full graphics, always. The game used to start in the reduced graphics mode meant for slow PCs of 1996 whenever its settings file said so: that is how GOG's own settings file is made, and a slow MiSTer does the same. You got poorer graphics, no shirt numbers 17-99 over the players' heads, and players with dark skin and blond hair drawn as a block of pixels. Now the game always starts in full graphics. If you want the old reduced mode, start the game with /H. In the reduced mode the two problems above are fixed too.
  • The real kits of 227 Italian clubs. Every Italian club from Serie A down to Serie D (227 clubs, checked one by one) now plays in its real 2026-27 kits: first and second shirt, shirt colours, shorts and socks. In Serie D many clubs had stand-in colours: now they are the right ones. Sources: the clubs' official shops and pages (Serie A to C), Wikipedia and the kit archives (Serie D).
  • Malen is no longer blond (Donyell Malen, Roma): the game has no bald heads, so he has dark hair, the closest look. The "pile of pixels" that appeared in his place in a match is fixed (it was the reduced graphics mode, see above).

Bigger squads, and buying from other clubs' extra players

  • Bigger squads. In the World 2026-27 career, the club you manage now starts with up to 30 real players (the 16 of its team record plus up to 14 more, the club's next most important players), instead of the 16 plus 10 invented reserves. Computer clubs still play with their 16. The extra players are never better than the weakest of the club's 16, and they are paid like reserves (66%) until they are transferred. The game's squad limit is still 30: a club that starts with 30 players has to sell before it can buy.
  • Buying other clubs' extra players. The national transfer market can now offer players 17-30 of other clubs, with price and wage from the mod's scale. When you buy one he joins your squad and cannot be bought a second time in that career.
  • Looking at other clubs' full squads. When you browse a club to buy, a second page (the button next to ESCI) lists its extra players. In STAT, VIS. SQUADRE (the squad view) now also shows a club's full squad, with a PAG. SUCC. button for the page of extras. These are for looking only: the computer club still plays with its 16.

Controls

  • A CONTROLS menu inside the game. OPTIONS now has a COMANDI / CONTROLS / COMMANDES / STEUERUNG button (between ESCI A DOS and ESCI). There you choose the input (keyboard only, joypad 1 + keyboard 2, keyboard 1 + joypad 2, two joypads), set each key by pressing it (a key already used is refused, ESC keeps the old one), calibrate the joypads (stick centred, then every corner, with a live test line) and save: the choice is written into the game's SETUP.DAT. If no joypad is connected the game goes on with the keyboard. The button is not there with SWOS++.
  • Joypad in keyboard mode (GOG). For joysticks that work in the menus but, in a match, only go up and left (two-axis sticks without a d-pad): the install page has a third tick, "GOG version, joypad in keyboard mode", off by default and used together with the first one. The stick, the d-pad and the buttons become the arrow keys and Ctrl; no calibration, any joystick or joypad works, and joypad and keyboard both control player 1. Untick it and install again to go back to the calibrated joypad.
  • Edit your shirt numbers. On the team screen before a match, press LEFT on one of your players to light his number: UP / DOWN go to the next / previous free number from 1 to 99, or type the digits (a number already in use is swapped with its owner); FIRE, ENTER, LEFT or RIGHT confirm, ESC cancels. It works only on your own team. The first change gives every player of the team his own number; from then on the team keeps its numbers (no automatic 12-16 on the bench, no swapping of numbers, new players get a free one). Careers and seasons save the numbers; in a friendly they last for the session.
  • Numbers of two digits are now drawn well on the tactics pitch, and in the bench list in a match (the number ends in one column, clear of the face icon, and the name starts a little further right).
  • The number over your player's head. Some players had no number over their head in a match, even when you controlled them, mostly on GOG and on MiSTer. It was a game start-up problem: the game left some of its memory not cleared and only DOSBox-X happens to clear it. Now the game clears it. In the reduced graphics mode (/H) the numbers 17-99 are drawn too.

Cups, tournaments and leagues

  • European places. A club that wins its league and the Conference League in the same season, and so already has a Champions League place, keeps the Champions League place (before, it was sent to the Europa League and its Champions League place was passed down). Reported by Pietro.
  • CONCACAF Champions Cup: a tie level on aggregate goes to away goals, then extra time (where away goals no longer count), then penalties, as in the real 2026 competition. The CAF Champions League and Confederation Cup ties your club plays decide on away goals and then penalties, as in 2025-26.
  • Guatemala and El Salvador now play their real 2026-27 season: two tournaments, the Apertura and the Clausura, each a double round robin of 12 clubs, points reset in between, and an annual table. Each tournament ends with its real final phase: the 3rd-6th and 4th-5th play-offs and the semi-finals over two legs (the better placed club goes through when level), then the final: over two legs in Guatemala, one match at a neutral ground in El Salvador, with extra time and penalties. Declared: the Clausura's calendar repeats the Apertura's with home and away swapped (the real 2027 dates are not out yet); when the regular phase ends level on points, the game's order applies (goal difference, goals scored).
  • Paraguay plays its real 2026 season: the Apertura and the Clausura, 22 match days each, home and away, on the real calendar, the Clausura from zero points. The leader of each table wins the tournament. Level on points, the order is decided by points in the matches between the clubs concerned (the "head-to-head"), then the head-to-head goal difference, the goal difference and the goals scored; an extra match at a neutral ground only if the clubs are still level on all of these. Declared: three or more clubs level at the top: no draw, the table's order after those criteria; no relegation (one division in the world).
  • Mexico: the right title on every screen. The Liga MX and Liga de Expansión tables and calendars are titled with the tournament being played (LIGA MX APERTURA, then LIGA MX CLAUSURA; MX APERTURA / MX CLAUSURA in the calendar).
  • The WINNERS page. For the play-offs that decide the title in your own league (for example Peru's Liga 1 play-offs, or Uruguay's CAMPEONATO URUGUAYO) it now says DA GIOCARE ("to be played") until the end of the season decides it, instead of leaving the line out. In Uruguay and Peru the champion is named as soon as one club leads both the Apertura and the Clausura (no play-off then).
  • Serie D play-offs. The FORMAT page of the Serie D groups and of the play-off now says clearly that the group play-offs decide placings only and give no promotion (a user who won them twice expected to go up).

Menus and screens

  • Club buttons in the lists. In the team choice of a career and in the club list of the transfer market, the buttons with the names of the Serie D clubs appeared in different, random colours. Now they all have their normal colour.
  • VISUALIZZA MONDO (view the world) in the historic worlds (World 2026-27, Europe 1984-85, 1996-97) opens straight on the world's continents (or countries), with no one-entry menu first.
  • Save names. A save called CON, NUL, PRN, AUX, COM1, LPT1 and the like (with or without an extension) used to write into the DOS device: no file, and the career was lost. The game now refuses the name with a message (NOME FILE NON VALIDO / INVALID FILENAME / NOM DE FICHIER INVALIDE / DATEINAME NICHT ERLAUBT) and goes back to the save box.
  • STATS menu. Coming back from FORMAT or WINNERS the menu no longer changes (before, extra entries appeared and an empty page 'NESSUNA DESCRIZIONE').
  • Data corrections (World 2026-27). The club AMERICA PE and TREFELIN B & G have their right names, 22 pairs of clubs with the same name are told apart, and 105 clubs that had no coach now have one. Further coaches are still missing for some clubs (El Salvador, Brazil, Australia, Italy, Suriname, Chinese Taipei).

Good to know

  • Saves. Careers saved with 4.0 or 4.0.1 load and play on. A career saved with 4.0.2 whose transfer market lists a player 17-30 of another club, or in which you have bought one, can only be loaded with 4.0.2 or later (older versions would read the player from the wrong place).
  • European qualifying rounds. If your club drops from the Champions League qualifying into the Conference League (or the Europa League) qualifying, the calendar shows the rounds already played under the new cup's name too (for example "PRELIM. ECL" for the first round). Only the label is wrong: the draw, the opponents and the places you earn are right. This will be fixed in 4.0.3.
  • Planned for 4.0.3: a nationality list for the coaches of each world, and bald players.
  • Reduced graphics: the reduced mode is now only on request (/H); on a very slow PC /F and the low-memory rule work as before.

Declared, as in 4.0.1

These were announced for 4.0.2 in the 4.0.1 notes and are not in this release; they stay as described there.

  • AFC Challenge League groups and AFC Champions League Elite league stage: still simulated (your club joins at the quarter-finals / the knockout).
  • Calendars not yet published (the Clausura 2027 of Mexico, Costa Rica, Honduras and Panama, and the last match days of Peru, Colombia and Uruguay): they repeat the first half's fixtures with home and away swapped.
  • Colombia's cuadrangulares: the real order of the group fixtures and the tie-break by regular-season place.
  • Single-match ties that end level (for example the Copa do Brasil final): extra time before the penalties, while the real rule goes straight to penalties.
  • Management record: for a season in which your club went out in a European qualifying round, it shows the qualifying competition with 'FINALIST' instead of the round.
  • Serie D groups with an odd number of clubs: if your club rests on the last match day, its Poule Scudetto or group play-off is decided by simulation.
  • Copa Libertadores / Sudamericana: the rounds that come after the domestic season has ended (the Sudamericana after the play-off, a group winner's or runner-up's round of 16 in February) are simulated at the season end.
