What's new in 4.0.1

Download the patcher

4.0.1 brings a joypad that works out of the box on GOG and, in the World 2026-27, more leagues in their real format (Mexico, Peru, Colombia, Uruguay), a nationality for every player, cups that follow promotions and relegations, line-ups by role, simpler menus and a safer install, plus fixes for some 4.0 bugs. Everything in 4.0 stays as it was; careers saved with 4.0 load and play on.

What's new

Joypad ready

  • Plug the joypad in and play. On a GOG install, the one-click install now sets everything up: the joypad is player 1 and the keyboard is player 2, with a ready calibration, and both the stick and the d-pad work. The keyboard still works as before.
  • No joypad, no problem. The game now checks at start whether a joypad is connected and goes to the keyboard automatically when there is none, so keyboard players do not have to change anything.
  • Your own settings. The install page has a tick, 'GOG version: I have a joypad / gamepad', already on for GOG: untick it to keep your own controls settings, and run the install again without the tick to put the originals back (the changed files are backed up).
  • Your own DOSBox (CD installs): its own box, 'Using your own DOSBox (CD, eXoDOS, other)?', is off by default. Tick it if you have a joypad (it changes only SETUP.DAT) and add three lines to your DOSBox configuration ([joystick], joysticktype=2axis, timed=false); mapping the d-pad and what to do in DOSBox-X are explained in the README and on the install page.

Mexico, exactly

  • Liga MX and the Liga de Expansión MX now play their real 2026-27 season. Each has two tournaments a season: the Apertura (the opening tournament) and the Clausura (the closing one). Each tournament is a single table, the Clausura starting again from zero points. The Apertura 2026 is played on its real match days (from Transfermarkt). Each tournament is followed by its own final phase, the liguilla (Mexican for the play-off): the top 8 go straight in (Liga MX has no play-in from the Apertura 2026), 1st v 8th, 2nd v 7th, 3rd v 6th, 4th v 5th, re-seeded semi-finals, all over two legs (a tie level on aggregate goes to the better placed club) and a final with extra time and penalties. Your club can play every phase.
  • Liga MX: the Campeón de Campeones. The winners of the Apertura and the Clausura meet in one match at a neutral ground (a draw goes to penalties, no extra time); a club that won both gets the trophy without a match. The Liga MX has held it every year since 2023 (sources: Mediotiempo, Fox Sports México, La Razón).
  • Liga de Expansión MX: 16 clubs, the same liguillas and its own Campeón de Campeones, over two legs. No promotion or relegation in 2026-27.
  • Declared:
    • The Clausura 2027 calendars are not published yet: each Clausura plays its Apertura's match days mirrored (home and away swapped) until the real ones are out.
    • Expansión's Campeón de Campeones over two legs (second leg at the better placed club, extra time and penalties) is our reading: no source gives its format for 2026-27 (the 2021-22 one went to penalties).
    • One Expansión Apertura match (Atlético La Paz v Leones Negros, match day 8) is missing from the published fixtures (Transfermarkt): it is played at Leones Negros, so every club has 7 home and 7 away matches.

South America: Peru, Colombia and Uruguay, the tournaments of the year

  • Peru (Liga 1, 18 clubs): the Apertura and the Clausura, 17 match days each, everybody once, on the real 2026 calendar; the Clausura starts again from zero points. Each tournament's table leader wins it. The accumulated table (Apertura + Clausura) is in STAT > ANNUAL TABLE. The national title goes to the Liga 1 play-offs: the two tournament winners (if they are in the accumulated top 8) and the accumulated table's top 2. A tournament winner in the top 2 waits in the final; with four clubs the semi-finals are 1st v 4th and 2nd v 3rd by the accumulated table. Everything is over two legs. A club that wins both tournaments is champion without playing.
  • Colombia (Primera A, 20 clubs): the Apertura, 19 match days on the real calendar. Its top 8 then play quarter-finals 1st v 8th, 4th v 5th, 2nd v 7th and 3rd v 6th, semi-finals and a final, all over two legs, with the second leg at the better placed club and penalties if level. The Finalización (Colombia's second tournament) is 19 match days from zero points. Its top 8 then play two cuadrangulares (groups of four, home and away): the 1st and the 2nd head the groups and the other six are drawn, three per group, as the Dimayor (Colombia's league organiser) draws them. The two group winners play a two-legged final.
  • Uruguay (16 clubs): the Apertura (15 match days, real calendar), then the Intermedio (the mid-year tournament), then the Clausura (15 match days, real calendar), each from zero points.
    • The Intermedio splits the clubs into two series by the Apertura table, in a snake order: series A = 1st, 4th, 5th, 8th, 9th, 12th, 13th, 16th. Each series is one round, and its winners meet in a final on neutral ground (extra time and penalties).
    • The annual table adds up all three tournaments.
    • The title: a single-match semi-final on neutral ground between the Apertura and the Clausura champions, then a two-legged final against the annual table's leader. The final is not played when the semi-final's winner leads the annual table. A club that wins both the Apertura and the Clausura is champion at once (the rules of the AUF, Uruguay's football association, for the coincidence of titles).
  • Your club can play every final phase. Every tournament's winner is listed on the WINNERS page.
  • Declared:
    • The Clausura and Finalización calendars are the Apertura's mirrored, match day by match day, as every published match day shows. The last match days not yet published (Peru 4, Colombia 2, Uruguay 3) follow that mirror. Their dates are a week apart: for Peru that ends on 22 November, the real last day.
    • Uruguay's Intermedio uses the game's own calendar of the two series, dated between the Apertura and the Clausura.
    • Colombia's cuadrangulares: the group fixtures are the game's, so the heads are not always at home on the last match day; clubs level on points in a group are separated by goal difference, where the Dimayor uses the better regular-season place.
    • Peru's play-offs: a tournament winner outside the accumulated top 8 gives its place to the next club of that table (the tournament rules do not say what happens); a tie level on aggregate goes to penalties, and the final's better placed club is at home in the second leg.
    • Relegation by promedio (the average of points over the last seasons: 3 seasons in Colombia, 2 in Uruguay) is not in. Peru and Colombia have one division in the world, so nobody goes down there. Uruguay relegates the last 3 of the end-of-season table.
    • Paraguay's two tournaments are not here yet (see "Declared for 4.0.2").

A nationality for every player

  • Players from 58 nations that the original game did not have now show their own nationality instead of the nearest one: Montenegro (MNE), Kosovo (KOS), Andorra (AND), Gibraltar (GIB), Kazakhstan (KAZ), Cape Verde (CPV), Curaçao (CUW), Haiti (HAI), Cuba (CUB), the Dominican Republic (DOM), Lebanon (LBN), Palestine (PLE), Hong Kong (HKG), Thailand (THA), Indonesia (IDN), Tahiti (TAH) and more. The nationality is the three-letter FIFA code, as in the original game, the same in every language, on the squad and transfer screens.
  • Rule: these nations have no national team in the game, so their players are not called up (before, a Montenegrin could be called up by Yugoslavia, an Andorran by Spain).
  • Careers of the 1996-97 game and of the Europe 1984-85 world are unchanged.

World 2026-27 cups

  • National and league cups follow promotions and relegations (World 2026-27). In 4.0 every cup kept the same clubs every season, so a promoted or relegated club never joined its new division's cup (Mestre, promoted to Serie C, played no Coppa Italia Serie C). Now, from the second season, each cup takes its clubs from the divisions as they are that season, with the real format: each division sends as many clubs, and gets as many byes, as in the real cup (the Coppa Italia Serie C: 5, 7 and 5 from the three Serie C groups; the Copa del Rey: 20 from LaLiga and 21 from LaLiga 2). Within a division, the clubs relegated from the division above come first, then the clubs that stayed, then the promoted ones, each by last season's final place; your club always takes part when its division does. The first season keeps the real 2026-27 draw. Every national and league cup of the world follows this rule (101 of 102); the one cup that includes a club from outside its country's league keeps its fixed list. The rule is on each cup's FORMAT page.
  • The main Coppa Italia keeps its real shape (its 16 clubs are from Serie A and Serie B), so a Serie C club does not enter it; the Coppa Italia Serie D is unchanged.
  • Saves: the clubs' final places are saved with the career. A 4.0 save loads and plays on; its first new season orders the clubs as the game lists them until a season has been completed.

Line-ups that make sense

  • The computer teams of the World 2026-27, of Europe 1984-85 and of the historic season packs (since 3.0) used to field their starters in the order of the team list, so the left-back stood at centre-back and the left midfielder in central midfield. Now each team's 11 starters follow their roles (goalkeeper, right-back, two centre-backs, left-back, right midfielder, two central midfielders, left midfielder, two forwards): 3,529 teams corrected. The original 1996-97 teams and the hand-written line-ups of the historic tourneys are unchanged.
  • In a career started before 4.0.1, the clubs the career already holds in memory keep their old line-up.

Menus and screens

  • Buying a foreign player now opens straight on the continents (World 2026-27) or on the nations (Europe 1984-85), without a CARR. STORICHE (CLASSIC CAREERS) menu in front (in the World 2026-27 that was a violet entry).
  • Career calendar: the two-line labels of the UEFA qualifying rounds are short enough for their line, e.g. "PRELIM. CL" over "TURNO 1 AND." (each language with its own round word).
  • UEFA qualifying rounds: when your club wins a qualifying tie, the next round now appears in the career calendar straight after the return leg. Before, it was added only when you went on to the next match, so for a while the calendar seemed to have no next round.
  • Match titles are fitted to the title bar by their real width: no title is cut any more (the 4.0 limit on long titles is gone) and none starts with a stray space. We checked the longest title of every competition.
  • WINNERS page: a competition's long name is shown in full when it fits (TROFEO DE CAMPEONES, not TROFEO CAMP.).

Install

  • GOG: GOG's own shortcut starts the English version. When the one-click install finds GOG's DOSBox, it writes one launcher per language beside the game: "SWOS Mod - Italiano.bat", "SWOS Mod - English.bat", "SWOS Mod - Francais.bat" and "SWOS Mod - Deutsch.bat" (for the languages GOG's folder has a configuration for). Start the game with them. A re-run writes back a launcher that was deleted.
  • A half-finished install is now caught twice. The one-click page shows "Writing the mod: i / N files... Do not close or reload the page", checks every file it wrote and ends with "Done: N / N files written and verified" or, in red, "INSTALL INCOMPLETE: only k of N files were written correctly ... choose the game folder again" (this part was already in the 23 October re-issue of 4.0). And if some World 2026-27 files are still missing, choosing the world (CLASSIC CAREERS, the green CAREER 2026-27 button, or Europe 1984-85) no longer quits to DOS: the game says "INCOMPLETE INSTALL: RUN THE PATCHER AGAIN" (in each language) and goes back to the menu.

Known issues of 4.0, revisited

  • European places: the 4.0 notes warned that in some seasons a European place could go to a club of the wrong country. This had already been fixed in 4.0 before its release and has not happened since: every country gets its right European places.
  • Serie D groups with an odd number of clubs: in such a group one club rests on each match day. If yours rests on the last one, it does not play the Poule Scudetto or its group play-off: they are decided by simulation. The 4.0 notes announced a fix for 4.0.1; the fix we tried could make the season impossible to finish, so it is planned for 4.0.2.
  • Copa Libertadores group thirds: in 4.0 their move to the Copa Sudamericana play-off was simulated. Now your club, third in its Libertadores group, plays the play-off (both legs, January). This works from your career's second season, because the first season's continental cups are the world's own. The rest of the Sudamericana, and the Libertadores round of 16 for a group winner or runner-up (both from February), are still simulated at the season end, because they come after the domestic season has ended (Argentina's ends in December). Planned for 4.0.2.
  • Serie D: a career saved after its Coppa Italia Serie D tie was drawn no longer starts the play-offs after a few match days when loaded (the tie is played; 4.0 saves affected are repaired on load). Gianmarco.
  • Careers saved during a season in which a play-off or title decider was settled without your club (for example Argentina's Apertura / Clausura knockouts when your club missed them, or Malta's decider when one club won both rounds) no longer start the next season with an empty league after loading (the match screen could not be played).
  • World 2026-27: a career saved with an earlier version no longer shows garbled titles on the European cup finals at the season end (seen in 4.0 too).
  • Menu buttons: the 4.0 notes mentioned a few wrong pixels on the menu buttons after a job offer. It was not a problem of the released 4.0 (it only showed up in a test version), so we took it off the list. If you see it, let us know.

Declared for 4.0.2

  • AFC Challenge League: its group stage (5 groups of 4, everybody once; the 5 winners and the 3 best runners-up go to the quarter-finals) is still simulated; your club joins at the quarter-finals. Playing the groups needs a ranking of the runners-up across the groups, which is not ready.
  • AFC Champions League Elite: its league stage (all the clubs in one table) is still simulated; it will be played in 4.0.2.
  • Guatemala, El Salvador and Paraguay: their real 44-match-day calendars (two tournaments a season) are longer than the game's calendar can hold today; they keep the format they had in 4.0 until then.
  • Calendars not yet published: the Clausura 2027 of Mexico, Costa Rica, Honduras and Panama, and the last match days of Peru, Colombia and Uruguay, repeat the first half's fixtures with home and away swapped until the real calendars are out.
  • Colombia's cuadrangulares: the real order of the group fixtures and the tie-break by regular-season place. Planned for 4.0.2.
  • Single-match ties: in some cups a single match (for example the Copa do Brasil final) that ends level goes to extra time before the penalties, while the real rule goes straight to penalties. This has been so since 3.2; it will follow the real rule in 4.0.2.
  • Management record: for a season in which your club went out in a European qualifying round, the record shows the qualifying competition with 'FINALIST' instead of the round in which it went out. It will show the round in 4.0.2.
  • Serie D rest day and the Libertadores continuation: see "Known issues of 4.0, revisited".
