What's new in 4.0.5

Download the patcher

4.0.5 brings the long benches awaited for 30 years (each competition has its real 2026-27 bench, up to 15 players), the national Super Cups in the World 2026-27 (nine countries, in their real format, played by your club when it qualifies) and the bald players, also awaited for 30 years, drawn by Marco. It also brings the real European draws and the real league calendars in the first season, the Mexican Campeón de Campeones in July, your own kits kept for good (SELECT KITS: ALWAYS), a list of coach nationalities for each world, New Zealand's championship phase and many fixes. Everything in 4.0, 4.0.1 and 4.0.2 stays as it was; careers saved with those versions load and play on.

What's new

In the spotlight: long benches, awaited for 30 years

Since 1996 SWOS has had 5 substitutes on the bench, and players have asked for more ever since. Awaited for 30 years, long benches are here: each competition has its real 2026-27 bench, up to 15 players, that scrolls in the match, with every player's name on the pitch.

  • Long benches in World 2026-27 careers. The bench is no longer always 5: every league, cup, play-off and continental cup uses its real 2026-27 rule (from the regulations or from real match sheets). Serie A 15; Serie B, LaLiga, the Brasileirão, the Argentine league and the UEFA and South American cups 12; Ligue 2 10; Premier League, Championship, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, MLS and Serie D 9; and so on. Where we have no data, the related league's bench or the confederation's usual bench applies. You can still make up to 5 substitutions, plus one more in extra time where the competition allows it (FA Cup, UEFA cups).
    • Before the match, the squad, line-up and tactics screens show the whole bench as a list that scrolls. At the end of the bench, pressing down on the last row jumps back to the top of the bench; pressing up from the top of the bench still goes to the starters (thanks to Marco for the idea).
    • In the match the dugout shows 5 players at a time and scrolls as you move down it; you can bring on any of them, and every player on the pitch shows his name, whatever his squad number.
    • The computer's clubs bring their full bench as well, their extra players included.
    • Friendlies, seasons, the historic worlds and the original 1996-97 game keep the bench of 5.
    • Highlights saved with a bench of more than 5 need 4.0.5 or later: an older version draws a yellow band where a player numbered 17 or above should show his name.

National Super Cups (World 2026-27)

A Super Cup is a match between the league champion and the national cup winner, played before the season's competitions start (or, in three countries, a small tournament). In 4.0.5 nine are played in your career, and your club plays its tie whenever it is one of the entrants. It appears in the calendar like any other cup, has its own paragraph on the FORMAT page of the national cup, and the trophy goes into your career record.

  • One match (a single match on neutral ground, 90 minutes then penalties, no extra time):
    • Supercoppa Italiana, 22 December: league champion v cup winner.
    • FA Community Shield (England), mid-August: league champion v FA Cup winner.
    • German Super Cup, late August: league champion v DFB-Pokal winner.
    • Trophée des Champions (France), mid-August: league champion v Coupe de France winner.
    • Supercopa do Brasil, late January: league champion v Copa do Brasil winner.
  • Final four (two semi-finals and a final in the same week, on neutral ground; the four entrants are the league champion, the cup winner, the cup finalist and the league runner-up; semi-finals: champion v cup finalist, and cup winner v league runner-up):
    • Supercopa de España, early February (semi-finals 2-3 February, final 6 February): 90 minutes then penalties.
    • Turkish Super Cup, early January (semi-finals 4-5 January, final 9 January): extra time (2 x 15 minutes), then penalties.
    • Saudi Super Cup, December (semi-finals 19-20 December, final 23 December): extra time, then penalties.
  • Who plays, every season. The entrants are decided by the rule above at the end of each season, from that season's real results; in the first season of the world they are the real 2026-27 ones (for example Inter v Lazio in Italy). A double (the same club wins the league and the cup) frees a place, which goes to: the cup finalist in Italy; the league runner-up in England, Germany, France and Brazil; the next club in the league (the 3rd) in Spain, Turkey and Saudi Arabia, in the final four's freed places.
  • When. In a new career the December-to-February ones are played already in the world's first season, with the real 2026-27 entrants. The summer ones (England, Germany, France, and Brazil's, which is in January) start from your second season, because the first season's entrants are the 2025-26 ones, which the game does not have.
  • Mexico: the Campeón de Campeones in July. Liga MX's Campeón de Campeones, until now played at the end of the season, is now played in July (25 July), from the second season: the winner of the Apertura against the winner of the Clausura of the season before, a single match on neutral ground, penalties if level. A club that won both tournaments gets the trophy without a match. The Liga de Expansión's stays at the end of the season, as before.
  • Declared (points that no source confirms or that we simplified):
    • All the Super Cups are played on neutral ground, 90 minutes then penalties unless stated above.
    • Italy: the date (22 December) and the venue come from the press, not official; the cup finalist taking a double's place is our reading.
    • Spain: the pairings follow the usual rule; the double rule (the next in the league) comes from an older source; both semi-finals are in one game week.
    • Turkey: which entrant meets which is inferred from the 2027 pairs; extra time follows the Turkish federation's last known rule; the venues are not announced; the double rule (league 3rd) is not verified.
    • Saudi Arabia: in the first season the field is the real 2026 one (Al-Hilal is not in it); from the second season the usual rule applies (champion v cup finalist, cup winner v league runner-up), not verified, and so is the double rule (league 3rd).
    • France: straight penalties are assumed (no source); after a PSG double, the league runner-up is the second entrant; the real host is drawn, here it is neutral.
    • Germany: the real match is at the second club's ground, here it is neutral.
    • England: from the second season the same week as 2026 (16 August), on neutral ground (2026 was at Cardiff).
    • Brazil: from the second season; the 2027 entrants are not known, so the rule decides; the venue is not known (neutral); penalties after 90 minutes and the league-runner-up double rule are not verified.
    • Mexico: from the second season, 25 July as in 2026 (played in the USA), on neutral ground; penalties after 90 minutes follow the pack's data.

Bald players, awaited for 30 years

Bald players too were awaited for 30 years: the game had always drawn a full head of hair on everyone. A very warm, heartfelt thank-you to Marco, who drew these heads for us, with patience and talent.

  • Two looks, 67 players. 67 real players of the World 2026-27 are now bald, with one of two looks: receding (a bald crown, hair at the sides) or shaved (a shaved head with its shading, drawn by Marco: pink with skin-tone edges on light skin, brown with a darker shade on dark skin). Among them Malen, Molina, Dimarco, Rüdiger, Koulibaly, Guirassy, M. Antonio, Niakhaté, Matić, T. Ream and Iličić. They are also bald on the squad and tactics lists, in the pre-match line-up and in Edit Teams. The pink is a new colour in the match; in the reduced graphics mode both shaved looks use the plain skin colour (no pink, no shade).
  • With SWOS++ the bald players keep their normal look (with hair) for now: the bald look works only without SWOS++.

Real fixtures in the first season (World 2026-27)

  • European cups. In the first season of the World 2026-27 the league phases of the Champions League, Europa League and Conference League use the real 2026-27 draws: all the pairings, home and away, and the order of the match days.
  • Leagues with the real calendar. In the first season of a World 2026-27 career these top divisions play the real 2026-27 calendar, every match day, home and away: Italy, England, Spain, Germany, France, Portugal, the Netherlands, Belgium, Turkey, Poland, Czech Republic, Greece, Romania, Ukraine, Russia, Serbia, Bulgaria, Israel, Denmark, Austria, Slovakia, Luxembourg, Wales, Scotland, Hungary, Northern Ireland, Andorra, Moldova, Croatia, Slovenia and Bosnia and Herzegovina (31 leagues). Argentina, the MLS and the Latin American leagues already had their real calendars. Later seasons are generated by the game.
  • Scotland and Northern Ireland, after the split. The Scottish Premiership now plays the real calendar in the first season too; after the split into two groups, home games follow the Scottish rule (the club that hosted that opponent fewer times plays at home). Northern Ireland is fixed as well: after the split, home games follow the official rule again (the club that was away in the third meeting plays at home).
  • North Macedonia. The first division now plays 4 rounds (36 matches), as in the official 2026-27 rules. The FORMAT page also describes the bottom of the table (9th and 10th go down, the 8th plays a one-match play-off at a neutral ground against the third of the second division, penalties if level): this is only described there.
  • Declared:
    • The dates follow the game's weekly spacing, not the exact real dates.
    • Leagues that split in two groups after a first stage (for example Denmark, Austria, Slovakia, Czech Republic, Greece, Romania, Serbia, Bulgaria, Israel, Hungary, Northern Ireland): only the regular season before the split is the real calendar; the rounds after the split depend on the table, so the game generates them.
    • North Macedonia keeps a generated calendar (with its 4 rounds).
    • Albania, Cyprus, Kosovo, Malta, Montenegro, San Marino, Gibraltar, Switzerland, Azerbaijan, Armenia, Liechtenstein and the leagues played in the calendar year (Norway, Sweden, Finland, Iceland, Ireland, Kazakhstan, Latvia, Lithuania, Faroe Islands, Estonia, Georgia, Belarus) keep a generated calendar for now: their full 2026-27 list is not published yet (or the season is already running).
    • A few matches can still move in real life (television choices): the lists are those published between June and August 2026, with the matches moved before 30 October 2026.

Careers

  • SELECT KITS: JUST THIS MATCH / ALWAYS. The kit editor has a new switch. In a career, ALWAYS keeps the kits you edit for your club: they stay in the following matches, in your saves and across seasons, and the season-end kit refresh never overwrites them. The default is JUST THIS MATCH, as before. How: SELECT KITS, edit your club's kit, choose ALWAYS, OK. The choice between first and second kit stays per match.
  • Coach nationality, per world. The list of nationalities a coach can have now depends on the world: CAREER (Europe 1984-85) offers the nations of that era (East Germany, USSR, West Germany, Yugoslavia...), CAREER 2026-27 the nations of today.
  • Serie C groups. At the end of each season the clubs coming up from Serie D or down from Serie B go into group A, B or C much as the Lega Pro does it. This approximates its criteria (geography, public order) from the real groups of the last ten seasons: northern clubs never go to group C, southern clubs never to group A, every region has a first and a second choice, and the U23 sides fill whichever group is short of a club. A club that came up from the Eccellenza keeps following its own region's groups in later seasons (reported by Enzo).
  • Milan Futuro is now Milan's B team: it can reach Serie B at most, and never plays in the same division as Milan.
  • Breno has its coach.
  • Serie D: FINISH SEASON and your group play-off. When your club's last league match is followed by another competition's day before its bye, FINISH SEASON no longer skips your group play-off: the menu comes back with MATCH and you play it.
  • Title play-offs. The champion of a title play-off you are not in (a final four, a finals series, the Serie D Poule Scudetto...) is now decided as your league ends: the WINNERS page shows it at the end of the season and it stays the same after CONTINUE. Fixed too (since 4.0): in a play-off with clubs from another division, that division's final table could change between the play-off draw and the season end.
  • Copa Libertadores, first qualifying round. A club that won the first qualifying round now plays both legs of the second (before: only the return leg, and leaving that screen could crash the game to DOS; since 4.0).
  • Kits for the Eccellenza reserve. The 42 Italian clubs of the Eccellenza (the level below Serie D) that can be promoted into Serie D (for example NOLA) now have their real kits; before they had an invented colour and white shorts. In a career already under way, a club that is already in YOUR Serie D group keeps its old kit until the current season ends; from the next season it has its real one.
  • Copa Libertadores. A club that finished 3rd in its group and dropped into the Copa Sudamericana could get your career stuck later in the season (the same match shown again and again). Fixed (it was there since 4.0.1).
  • Coppa Italia Serie D in older careers. Loading a career saved with a 4.0.x version while a Coppa Italia Serie D tie was waiting showed a wrong competition name ('RV. TITOLO') in the fixture list. Fixed.
  • Coppa Italia Serie D. When your club was not in the last rounds, the final was always played by the same two clubs of the preliminary round, so the same club won the cup every season. Now the real semi-final winners play the final (found by Ben, of OpenSWOS).
  • Italian kits: 20 clubs fixed. CATANIA now plays in red-and-azure stripes (reported by Salvatore). Stripes are back for Catanzaro (home and away), Juve Stabia, Carrarese, Pisa (away), Carpi (away), Pescara and Grosseto, and Teramo, Ossese, Venafro, Bra, Millesimo, Scanzorosciate, Legnago Salus, Pro Sesto, Mezzolara, Monastir, Certosa Campagnano and Real Forio now have striped shirts. Where two kits still look alike, the game uses its plain emergency kit, as happens in real life.
  • An away kit against every opponent. Every club of Serie A to Serie D and of the Eccellenza reserve now has a usable away kit against every possible opponent (second kits added for Sansepolcro, Angelana, Venafro and Nola).
  • Kits of careers started earlier. If you started a career with an earlier version, at the season end the club you manage takes this version's kits too (for example Ischia), unless you changed them yourself (thanks Francesco).
  • Transfers: more players in one deal. The "+" on an offer (to add a second player of the same club) now has NEXT PAGE too, so a club's players 17-30 can be added to the deal (thanks to Enzo).
  • Play-offs and play-outs: who goes through on a level tie. When a level tie is decided by the better league placing (Italian, Japanese, Argentine, Central American and other play-offs and play-outs), the result line now says who goes through and why, for example "A.E.T. - LIGORNA THROUGH (BETTER PLACED)", instead of only "A.E.T." or nothing. It also fixes two-leg ties level on aggregate, which wrongly said "WIN 2-2 ON AGG.". The same line shows in the round results list.

Menus and screens

  • WINNERS page for every league. It now shows this season and last season for every league, the league your club actually played in.
  • Super Cup on the WINNERS page, all season. If your club lost its final-four semi-final, the final is simulated and its winner is shown.
  • PHASE 1 page for leagues with a split. After the split it opens by itself, until the first result of the second phase; in STAT you switch between PHASE 1 and GROUPS.
  • Shirt numbers on the bench. A team with real squad numbers above 16 now keeps them on the bench in every mode (friendlies, seasons, preset competitions; careers already did), also for teams edited with external editors. When a duplicated number is fixed, the starter keeps his (reported by Marco).
  • Names of 14 characters in the match. Player names of up to 14 characters now show in the match: write ROBERTO_CARLOS and it appears as 'ROBERTO CARLOS' (the '_' joins two words); a single long surname such as SCHWEINSTEIGER shows in full. A first name plus a surname of 12 to 14 letters is still shortened: use the '_' to join them (thanks to Marco and Francesco for the tip).
  • Double first names. For 772 players whose name starts with two first names, the two are now joined, so the match shows the surname: MARC_OLIVER KEMPF appears as 'KEMPF', not 'OLIVER KEMPF'. On the squad screens the joiner looks like a space.
  • National top scorers: INT column. The national top scorers table has a new INT column with the goals scored in the international cups (Champions League, Europa League, Libertadores...); before, they only counted in the total. Goals scored in play-offs, play-outs, title play-offs, the NZ Grand Final, the national supercups and the Coppa Italia Serie D now count in the scorers table (league (LGE), cup 1 (CUP1) and cup 2 (CUP2) columns) and in the total; before, they were lost and changed the scorer's form (they could even mark a reserve as a trial player). Goals in New Zealand's championship group and Italy's Poule Scudetto now count as league goals instead of international ones (thanks to Luigi for the scorers report).
  • REGGINA now plays in amaranto (maroon).
  • Title play-offs with groups and knockouts (the Poule Scudetto and others): the final is now called FINALE (it was called SEMIFINALE).
  • Job offer letters. After reading a job offer letter, a few stray pixels damaged the menus' background until the next match (since 4.0). Fixed.
  • Earlier stages in STAT. STAT has separate entries for the earlier stages of the season (Mexico: MX APERTURA; Argentina: L.P. APERTURA; Uruguay: PR. APERTURA, PR. INTERMEDIO...), each showing that stage's final table with its own zone colours, for example during the Clausura. Argentina's earlier tournament table shows the ZONA A and ZONA B headings, with each zone's top 8 in green.
  • Table colours skip B teams and reserves. The colours of the tables now skip B teams and reserve sides that cannot be promoted or reach the play-offs: if one finishes in a coloured place it stays uncoloured and the next club takes the colour (Spain, Portugal, Germany, the Netherlands, New Zealand; Italy's B teams only if their first team is in the division above). In New Zealand's National League Championship the two green places always go to the top two clubs that can reach the Grand Final, never to an A-League reserve side.
  • Fixture list, home team first. The career fixture list now shows the home team first, as real fixture lists do (for example CHIVAS v AMERICA).
  • Job offer, VIEW SQUAD. It shows the whole squad: page 2 has players 17-30.
  • Menus aligned. The gap between CONTROLS and EXIT in the main menu is now even (reported by Marco). In the shirt-number editor the green background is filled, and the shadow of the big numbers is visible there and on the tactics screen.
  • CONTROLS: one device choice removed. The 'P1 KEYBOARD, P2 JOYPAD' choice is gone: the game always played it as P1 JOYPAD, P2 KEYBOARD, swapping the two players' devices. A file saved with it now shows P1 JOYPAD, P2 KEYBOARD (reported by Marco).
  • SEASON WINNERS: Serie D title round. The SEASON WINNERS page calls the Serie D title round POULE SCUDETTO SERIE D (its own name, as in the calendar), so it can't be mistaken for the Serie D group play-offs.

Install

  • The "SWOS 16/17" package. The install page recognises that package and explains, in the four languages, how to install: with the original CD that comes in the package, or with the GOG version (reported by Francesco and Paolo).

Good to know

  • Saves. Careers saved with 4.0, 4.0.1 and 4.0.2 load and play on. In a career saved with an earlier version the Super Cups start from the first season end after loading.
  • Older careers: a career started with an earlier version has no PHASE 1 page and no Super Cup line on the WINNERS page; a club that changed division in such a career sees last season's WINNERS page with this season's league name.
  • Saves made with the original game can freeze at a season end; the fix is planned for 4.2 (reported by Libero).
  • Where to find a Super Cup. It shows in your fixture list, from the start of the season, if your club is one of its entrants. VIEW COMPETITIONS has a fifth entry for it (also once it has been played), and the national cup's FORMAT page describes it.
  • New Zealand: the National League championship phase. It is played as in 2026: 11 clubs (4 Northern, 3 Central, 2 Southern, plus the reserve teams of Auckland FC and Wellington Phoenix), a single round robin, then a one-match Grand Final between the top two clubs that are not reserve teams (the reserves cannot reach it), with extra time and penalties. Your club plays all its matches. Declared:
    • the order of the matches is generated (balanced), not the real 2026 draw;
    • a tie in the table is decided by points, goal difference and goals scored only (no head-to-head, discipline or coin toss);
    • if your club rested in the last round of the championship group, the season used to be able to end without letting you play the Grand Final, or to get stuck: fixed, the final is now always played when your club is in it;
    • if your club misses the Grand Final while resting in the last Championship group round, the season may still show it playing a final that does not count; the real champion is correct;
    • the Grand Final is played at the ground of the higher-placed club (in reality the federation chooses the venue);
    • the dates start the week after the regional league, one match a week; if your club is still playing in the OFC Champions League knockouts, the championship starts right after your last OFC match instead (in reality the two do not overlap). Clubs not in the OFC knockouts play on these dates.
  • Bald players: only the 67 players marked; more can be marked in future versions.
  • SWOS++. The green CAREER 2026-27 button is always in the main menu, with SWOS++ or not. Under SWOS++, SWOS++'s own entry appears at the top of the main menu in place of SAVE while no competition or career is loaded; when one is loaded, CONTINUE and SAVE come back and the SWOS++ entry is hidden. The CONTROLS button is not there with SWOS++ (SWOS++ has its own controls), and the bald players keep their normal look.
  • Title play-offs, Italy. A few promotion play-offs of other divisions (Italy) are still decided when you press CONTINUE.
  • Two players on one keyboard with separate key sets are not in this version; planned for 4.0.6.
