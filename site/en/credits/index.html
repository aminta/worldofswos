Credits and data sources

Where the data comes from, and the people who helped.

  • Squads, market values, coaches, league members 2026-27 and tables 2025-26: Transfermarkt (transfermarkt.com).
  • Player attributes: EA SPORTS FC ratings (ea.com), FC 26-era data.
  • Kits: Wikipedia contributors (CC BY-SA 4.0); club to article links from Wikidata (CC0).
  • UEFA association and club coefficients: Bert Kassies' UEFA coefficients site (kassiesa.net).
  • National cups 2026-27 (rounds played and drawn): Transfermarkt.
  • League and cup formats: the competitions' own regulations (e.g. the Liga Profesional's 2026 regulation, the FAW's 2026-27 Cymru Premier format, the Austrian Bundesliga's 2026-27 decisions) and their Wikipedia season pages.
  • The game files hold derived data only: the squads, skills and prices are computed from these sources (the game's own price scale); no market values, ratings or source pages are shipped.

Also in this release

  • Europe 1984-85 career: league tables in colour (see above), each competition's FORMAT page with its table colours, the European cups' titles without a leading space.
  • Long competition titles no longer overwrite the game's memory (the title field holds 34 characters): they are shortened to their usual short form, which also fixes the classic season DDR 1988-89's European cup titles.
  • CLASSIC CAREERS no longer lists ALBANIA as an entry of its own (since 3.0 the first country of the Europe 1984-85 world also appeared next to the world).
  • The 1996-97 game as it really was (the real 1996-97 groups, Supercup, Intertoto, leagues and cups) comes in 4.1.

Updating

  • One-click install (Chrome or Edge): choose the game folder (GOG, CD install, eXoDOS / DOSBox; Mac GOG: the .harddisk folder inside the app) and the patcher recognises the version, keeps the files it changes in MOD_BACKUP\4.0 and writes the mod in place; from the CD it copies the game into a new folder first. Run it again to update. The page is in 4 languages (IT / EN / FR / DE); the manual download stays for other browsers.
  • The patcher upgrades every published version (1.0.1 to 3.3.6, CD and GOG, also the English SWOS++ version) and the original game, checking every file before and after (an old file goes back to the original, then to 4.0). Drop your executable and DATA files as usual: the patcher writes every file the World 2026-27 needs (its team files and squads beyond 16, the waiting clubs' pools, the continental guests' team files, its world files, the FORMAT texts and the table colours in 4 languages) and the changed originals (the executables, TEAM files, the renamed EUROCUP.TMD, UEFACUP.TMD and POOLPLYR.DAT). Copy the new files into DATA too, or use the one-click install. The patcher page now needs a recent browser (Chrome 103+, Safari 16.4+, Firefox 113+).
  • Saves: careers of the 1996-97 game saved with 3.3.6 and earlier load and play on. Careers of the Europe 1984-85 world saved with 3.x load and play on too.
