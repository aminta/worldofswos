Credits and data sources

Where the data comes from, and the people who helped.

  • Squads, market values, coaches, league members 2026-27 and tables 2025-26: Transfermarkt (transfermarkt.com).
  • Player attributes: EA SPORTS FC ratings (ea.com), FC 26-era data.
  • Kits: Wikipedia contributors (CC BY-SA 4.0); club to article links from Wikidata (CC0).
  • UEFA association and club coefficients: Bert Kassies' UEFA coefficients site (kassiesa.net).
  • National cups 2026-27 (rounds played and drawn): Transfermarkt.
  • League and cup formats: the competitions' own regulations (e.g. the Liga Profesional's 2026 regulation, the FAW's 2026-27 Cymru Premier format, the Austrian Bundesliga's 2026-27 decisions) and their Wikipedia season pages.
  • The game files hold derived data only: the squads, skills and prices are computed from these sources (the game's own price scale); no market values, ratings or source pages are shipped.
