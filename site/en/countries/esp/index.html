SPAIN

SPAIN: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

  • LALIGA

    20 clubs · double round robin, 38 matchdays · 3 down

  • SEGUNDA DIVISION

    22 clubs · double round robin, 42 matchdays · 2 up · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

    • PLAY-OFF DE ASCENSO promotion play-off: SEGUNDA DIVISION 3rd-6th; round 1: two legs, home order as in the real schedule, extra time, then the better placed; round 2: two legs, the better placed at home (2nd leg), extra time, then the better placed; the winner to LALIGA
  • COPA DEL REYCup

    41 clubs · from the round of 64 · the clubs still in the real 2026-27 cup, 0 of the 9 first-round pairs as really drawn · 23 byes into the 2nd round · rounds 1-4: single matches, extra time and penalties · round 5: two legs (aggregate, then extra time and penalties) · final: single match, extra time and penalties

  • Europe

    season 1: the real 2026-27 league-phase clubs (Champions League Real Betis, Real Madrid, Atleti, Barcelona, Villarreal; Europa League Real Sociedad, Celta; Conference League Getafe) · from season 2: Champions League 4, Europa League 2, Conference League 1 by the simulated association coefficients
