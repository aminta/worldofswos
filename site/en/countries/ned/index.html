HOLLAND

HOLLAND: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

  • EREDIVISIE

    18 clubs · double round robin, 34 matchdays · 2 down

    • PLAY-OFFS PROMOTIE/DEGRADATIE promotion play-off: EERSTE DIVISIE 3rd-8th + EREDIVISIE 16th; round 1: two legs, home order as in the real schedule, extra time and penalties; round 2: two legs, home order as in the real schedule, extra time and penalties; round 3: two legs, the better placed at home (2nd leg), extra time and penalties; round 4: two legs, home order as in the real schedule, extra time and penalties; the winner to EREDIVISIE
    • PLAY-OFFS EUROPEES VOETBAL European play-off: EREDIVISIE 5th-8th; 2 rounds of single match, the better placed at home, extra time and penalties
  • EERSTE DIVISIE

    20 clubs · double round robin, 38 matchdays · 2 up · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

  • KNVB BEKERCup

    34 clubs · from the round of 64 · the clubs still in the real 2026-27 cup, all 2 first-round pairs as really drawn · 30 byes into the 2nd round · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties

  • Europe

    season 1: the real 2026-27 league-phase clubs (Champions League PSV, Feyenoord; Europa League AZ Alkmaar, N.E.C.; Conference League Twente, Ajax) · from season 2: Champions League 2, Europa League 2, Conference League 1 by the simulated association coefficients
