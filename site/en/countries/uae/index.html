U.A.E.

U.A.E.: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

Continental places (season 1)
  • AFC CHAMPIONS LEAGUE ELITE: 4 (1st-3rd, cup winner)
  • AFC CHAMPIONS LEAGUE TWO: 1 (best placed not yet qualified)
  • UAE PRO LEAGUE

    14 clubs · double round robin, 26 matchdays · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

  • UAE PRESIDENT'S CUPCup

    14 clubs · from the round of 16 · the clubs still in the real 2026-27 cup, 0 of the 6 first-round pairs as really drawn · 2 byes into the 2nd round · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties

  • UAE LEAGUE CUPLeague cup

    14 clubs · every club of the league, drawn by the game · 2 byes into the 2nd round · two legs (aggregate, then extra time and penalties) · final: single match, extra time and penalties

The in-game FORMAT page

As the game shows it (FORMAT button of the competition).

UAE PRO LEAGUE
THE LEAGUE HAS 14 CLUBS WHO PLAY EACH OTHER HOME AND AWAY: 26 MATCHDAYS. A WIN IS WORTH 3 POINTS, A DRAW 1.

THE 13TH-14TH WOULD GO DOWN TO FIRST DIVISION, WHICH IS NOT IN THE WORLD: HERE NOBODY GOES DOWN.

TABLE COLOURS:
LIGHT BLUE 1ST-4TH: AFC CHAMPIONS LEAGUE ELITE
GREEN 5TH: AFC CHAMPIONS LEAGUE TWO
THE CUP WINNER: AFC CHAMPIONS LEAGUE ELITE
UAE PRESIDENT'S CUP
THE REAL CUP HAS 15 CLUBS, WHO ENTER AT DIFFERENT ROUNDS BY DIVISION. IN THE GAME 14 OF THEM ARE LEFT, KNOCKOUT. 2 OF THEM GO STRAIGHT TO THE SECOND ROUND.

THE FIRST ROUND, THE QUARTER-FINALS, THE SEMI-FINALS AND THE FINAL ARE PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE FIRST ROUND FOLLOWS THE REAL DRAW.
UAE LEAGUE CUP
IN THE GAME IT STARTS WITH 14 CLUBS, KNOCKOUT. 2 OF THEM GO STRAIGHT TO THE SECOND ROUND.

THE FIRST ROUND, THE QUARTER-FINALS AND THE SEMI-FINALS ARE PLAYED OVER TWO LEGS, WITH THE AWAY GOALS RULE. THE FINAL IS PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE FIRST ROUND FOLLOWS THE REAL DRAW.
