  • KUP SRBIJECup

    14 clubs · from the round of 16 · the clubs still in the real 2026-27 cup, 1 of the 6 first-round pairs as really drawn · 2 byes into the 2nd round · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties

The in-game FORMAT page

As the game shows it (FORMAT button of the competition).

SUPERLIGA SRBIJE
THE LEAGUE HAS 14 CLUBS WHO PLAY EACH OTHER HOME AND AWAY: 26 MATCHDAYS. A WIN IS WORTH 3 POINTS, A DRAW 1.

AFTER 26 MATCHDAYS THE TABLE SPLITS: THE CLUBS FROM 1ST TO 7TH PLAY ONCE MORE, KEEPING ALL THE POINTS OF THE FIRST PHASE, THE CLUBS FROM 8TH TO 14TH PLAY ONCE MORE, KEEPING ALL THE POINTS OF THE FIRST PHASE.

NOBODY GOES DOWN.

EUROPEAN PLACES BY THE ASSOCIATION UEFA RANKING (UEFA ACCESS LIST): THE 2026-27 TABLE FOR THE 2027-28 CUPS, REAL 2026 RANKING:
LIGHT BLUE 1ST: CHAMPIONS LEAGUE
DARK BLUE 2ND-3RD: CONFERENCE LEAGUE
THE CUP WINNER: EUROPA LEAGUE
FROM THE NEXT SEASON ON: SIMULATED RANKING
THE 2 EUROPEAN PERFORMANCE SPOTS AND THE TITLE HOLDERS REBALANCING ARE DECIDED AT THE SEASON END, NOT COLOURED IN ADVANCE
KUP SRBIJE
THE REAL CUP HAS 36 CLUBS, WHO ENTER AT DIFFERENT ROUNDS BY DIVISION. IN THE GAME 14 OF THEM ARE LEFT, KNOCKOUT. 2 OF THEM GO STRAIGHT TO THE SECOND ROUND.

THE FIRST ROUND, THE QUARTER-FINALS, THE SEMI-FINALS AND THE FINAL ARE PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE FIRST ROUND FOLLOWS THE REAL DRAW.
