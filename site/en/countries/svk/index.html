As the game shows it (FORMAT button of the competition).

1. LIGA
THE LEAGUE HAS 12 CLUBS WHO PLAY EACH OTHER HOME AND AWAY: 22 MATCHDAYS. A WIN IS WORTH 3 POINTS, A DRAW 1.

AFTER 22 MATCHDAYS THE TABLE SPLITS: CHAMPIONSHIP GROUP (1ST-6TH) PLAYS HOME AND AWAY, KEEPING ALL THE POINTS OF THE FIRST PHASE, RELEGATION GROUP (7TH-12TH) PLAYS HOME AND AWAY, KEEPING ALL THE POINTS OF THE FIRST PHASE.

THE 12TH WOULD GO DOWN TO 2. LIGA, WHICH IS NOT IN THE WORLD, THE 11TH WOULD PLAY THE RELEGATION PLAY-OFF: HERE NOBODY GOES DOWN.

SLOVENSKY POHAR
THE REAL CUP HAS 128 CLUBS, WHO ENTER AT DIFFERENT ROUNDS BY DIVISION. IN THE GAME 11 OF THEM ARE LEFT, KNOCKOUT. 5 OF THEM GO STRAIGHT TO THE SECOND ROUND.

THE FIRST ROUND, THE QUARTER-FINALS, THE SEMI-FINALS AND THE FINAL ARE PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE FIRST ROUND FOLLOWS THE REAL DRAW.
