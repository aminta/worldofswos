SLOVAKIA

SLOVAKIA: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

  • SLOVAK FIRST FOOTBALL LEAGUE

    12 clubs · double round robin (22 matchdays) · after 22 matchdays: 1-6 double round robin, 7-12 double round robin · 32 matchdays · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

  • SLOVENSKY POHARCup

    11 clubs · from the round of 16 · the clubs still in the real 2026-27 cup, 0 of the 3 first-round pairs as really drawn · 5 byes into the 2nd round · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties

  • Europe

    season 1: the real 2026-27 league-phase clubs (Champions League S. Bratislava) · from season 2: Conference League 1 by the simulated association coefficients
