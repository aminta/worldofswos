MALTA

MALTA: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

No direct continental place in season 1.

  • MALTESE PREMIER LEAGUE

    12 clubs · single round robin (11 matchdays) · stage 1: after 11 matchdays: 1-6 single round robin, 7-12 single round robin · stage 2: after 16 matchdays: points and figures reset to 0 (the Opening Round table is kept), 1-12 single round robin · home order: the return legs of the first meeting · stage 3: after 27 matchdays: 1-6 single round robin, 7-12 single round robin · 32 matchdays · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

  • MALTESE FA TROPHYCup

    12 clubs · every club of the league, drawn by the game · 4 byes into the 2nd round · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties · rounds numbered as in reality

  • Europe

    season 1: no club in the league phases

The in-game FORMAT page

As the game shows it (FORMAT button of the competition).

MALTESE PREMIER LEAGUE
THE LEAGUE HAS 12 CLUBS WHO PLAY EACH OTHER HOME AND AWAY: 22 MATCHDAYS. A WIN IS WORTH 3 POINTS, A DRAW 1.

AFTER 11 MATCHDAYS THE TABLE SPLITS: THE CLUBS FROM 1ST TO 6TH PLAY ONCE MORE, KEEPING ALL THE POINTS OF THE FIRST PHASE, THE CLUBS FROM 7TH TO 12TH PLAY ONCE MORE, KEEPING ALL THE POINTS OF THE FIRST PHASE.

AFTER 16 MATCHDAYS THE TABLE SPLITS: THE CLUBS FROM 1ST TO 12TH PLAY ONCE MORE, KEEPING ALL THE POINTS OF THE FIRST PHASE.

AFTER 27 MATCHDAYS THE TABLE SPLITS: THE CLUBS FROM 1ST TO 6TH PLAY ONCE MORE, KEEPING ALL THE POINTS OF THE FIRST PHASE, THE CLUBS FROM 7TH TO 12TH PLAY ONCE MORE, KEEPING ALL THE POINTS OF THE FIRST PHASE.

THE 11TH-12TH WOULD GO DOWN TO CHALLENGE LEAGUE, WHICH IS NOT IN THE WORLD: HERE NOBODY GOES DOWN.

EUROPEAN PLACES BY THE ASSOCIATION UEFA RANKING (UEFA ACCESS LIST): THE 2026-27 TABLE FOR THE 2027-28 CUPS, REAL 2026 RANKING:
LIGHT BLUE 1ST: CHAMPIONS LEAGUE
DARK BLUE 2ND-3RD: CONFERENCE LEAGUE
THE CUP WINNER: CONFERENCE LEAGUE
FROM THE NEXT SEASON ON: SIMULATED RANKING
THE 2 EUROPEAN PERFORMANCE SPOTS AND THE TITLE HOLDERS REBALANCING ARE DECIDED AT THE SEASON END, NOT COLOURED IN ADVANCE
PREMIER LEAGUE FINAL FOUR
THE TOP 4 OF THE LEAGUE'S PHASES PLAY THE FINAL FOUR ON NEUTRAL GROUND: SINGLE-MATCH SEMI-FINALS AND FINAL, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES. THE WINNER IS THE CHAMPION OF MALTA.
MALTESE FA TROPHY
THE REAL CUP HAS 39 CLUBS, WHO ENTER AT DIFFERENT ROUNDS BY DIVISION. IN THE GAME 12 OF THEM ARE LEFT, KNOCKOUT. 4 OF THEM GO STRAIGHT TO THE SECOND ROUND.

THE FIRST ROUND, THE QUARTER-FINALS, THE SEMI-FINALS AND THE FINAL ARE PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL.
