Every country, every competition

91 countries and every playable competition, with its format, as the World 2026-27 plays it. Generated from the mod's own data.

Continental competitions

  • UEFA

    UEFA Champions League · UEFA Europa League · UEFA Conference League
    League phase of 36 with one table, knockout play-offs and the seeded bracket, the 2026-27 qualifying rounds, no away goals.

  • CONMEBOL

    Copa Libertadores (47 clubs: three two-legged qualifying rounds, 8 groups of 4, two-legged round of 16, quarter- and semi-finals, a single-match final), Copa Sudamericana (56 clubs: a single-match first round between clubs of the same association, 8 groups of 4, a two-legged play-off against the Libertadores group thirds, two-legged round of 16 to semi-finals, a single-match final), Recopa (the two winners, over two legs).

  • CONCACAF

    Champions Cup (two-legged first round, round of 16, quarter- and semi-finals: away goals, then penalties; a single-match final at the finalist with the better goal difference in the earlier rounds).

  • AFC

    Champions League Elite (a single-match preliminary round; a league stage in two regions, West and East, of 16 clubs: 4 pots of 4, 8 matches each, 2 against each pot, the real 2026-27 pots and fixtures; two-legged round of 16; single-match quarter-finals, semi-finals and final; level ties go to extra time and penalties), Champions League Two (preliminary round, 8 groups of 4, two-legged round of 16 to semi-finals, a single-match final), Challenge League (preliminary round, 5 groups of 4 with the best runners-up also through, two-legged quarter- and semi-finals, a single-match final).

  • CAF

    Champions League and Confederation Cup (two-legged first and second qualifying rounds, 4 groups of 4, two-legged quarter-finals, semi-finals and final: away goals, then penalties, no extra time; only the real 3 byes skip the first round: RS Berkane, Esperance and the holder Mamelodi Sundowns; a holder that qualifies through its league uses its own place), Super Cup (the two winners, a single match at a neutral ground, penalties).

  • OFC

    Champions League (a qualifying group of 4 with the champions of the four lowest-ranked associations, 2 groups of 4, single-match semi-finals and final at a centralised host; one place per association, New Zealand included).

  • FIFA

    Intercontinental Cup, the real 2026 stepladder, all single matches with extra time and penalties: the African-Asian-Pacific play-off (the AFC winner v the OFC champion, alternating with CAF), then the African-Asian-Pacific Cup (the CAF winner v the play-off winner, at the club of the higher FIFA-ranked country) and the Derby of the Americas (the Libertadores winner v the CONCACAF winner, at a neutral ground), the FIFA Challenger Cup between their winners, and the final against the UEFA Champions League winner.
