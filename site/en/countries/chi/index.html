CHILE

CHILE: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

Continental places (season 1)
  • COPA LIBERTADORES: 4 (1st-3rd, cup winner)
  • COPA SUDAMERICANA: 4 (next 4 best placed not yet qualified)
  • LIGA DE PRIMERA

    16 clubs · double round robin, 30 matchdays · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

  • COPA CHILECup

    10 clubs · from the round of 16 · the clubs still in the real 2026 cup, all 2 first-round pairs as really drawn · 6 byes into the 2nd round · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties

  • COPA DE LA LIGALeague cup

    16 clubs · every club of the league, drawn by the game · rounds 1-2: single matches, extra time and penalties · round 3: two legs (aggregate, then extra time and penalties) · final: single match, extra time and penalties

The in-game FORMAT page

As the game shows it (FORMAT button of the competition).

LIGA DE PRIMERA
THE LEAGUE HAS 16 CLUBS WHO PLAY EACH OTHER HOME AND AWAY: 30 MATCHDAYS. A WIN IS WORTH 3 POINTS, A DRAW 1.

THE 15TH-16TH WOULD GO DOWN TO PRIMERA B, WHICH IS NOT IN THE WORLD: HERE NOBODY GOES DOWN.

TABLE COLOURS:
LIGHT BLUE 1ST-3RD: COPA LIBERTADORES
GREEN 4TH-7TH: COPA SUDAMERICANA
THE CUP WINNER: COPA LIBERTADORES
COPA CHILE
THE REAL CUP HAS 32 CLUBS, WHO ENTER AT DIFFERENT ROUNDS BY DIVISION. IN THE GAME 10 OF THEM ARE LEFT, KNOCKOUT. 6 OF THEM GO STRAIGHT TO THE SECOND ROUND.

THE FIRST ROUND, THE QUARTER-FINALS, THE SEMI-FINALS AND THE FINAL ARE PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE FIRST ROUND FOLLOWS THE REAL DRAW.
COPA DE LA LIGA
IN THE GAME IT STARTS WITH 16 CLUBS, KNOCKOUT.

THE ROUND OF 16 AND THE QUARTER-FINALS ARE PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE SEMI-FINALS ARE PLAYED OVER TWO LEGS, WITH PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE FINAL IS PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE FIRST ROUND FOLLOWS THE REAL DRAW.
