POULE SCUDETTO SERIE D
THE 9 WINNERS OF THE SERIE D GROUPS PLAY THREE TRIANGULARS (GROUPS A-B-C, D-E-F, G-H-I), SINGLE ROUND. THE 3 WINNERS AND THE BEST RUNNER-UP PLAY DRAWN TWO-LEGGED SEMI-FINALS: POINTS, THEN GOALS, NO AWAY GOALS, THEN PENALTIES, NO EXTRA TIME. THE FINAL IS A SINGLE MATCH ON NEUTRAL GROUND, NO EXTRA TIME, THEN PENALTIES. THE WINNER IS ITALIAN AMATEUR CHAMPION.
COPPA ITALIA SERIE D
EVERY SERIE D CLUB, ON THE REAL 2026-27 BRACKET: PRELIMINARY ROUND, FIRST ROUND, THEN TO THE FINAL. SINGLE MATCH, NO EXTRA TIME: LEVEL, PENALTIES. FROM SEASON 2 THE CLUBS NEW TO THE SERIE D PLAY THE PRELIMINARY ROUND.

THE BOTTOM 3 GO DOWN. THE 17TH GOES DOWN DIRECTLY IF THE 16TH IS MORE THAN 4 POINTS CLEAR, OTHERWISE THE 16TH AND 17TH PLAY OUT OVER TWO LEGS, THE FIRST AT THE 17TH: IF LEVEL THE 16TH STAYS UP, BUT CLUBS LEVEL ON LEAGUE POINTS GO TO EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES.
PLAY-OFF SERIE C
THE FOURTH CLUB GOING UP TO SERIE B COMES FROM A 28-CLUB PLAY-OFF: THE 2ND TO 10TH OF THE THREE GROUPS AND THE COPPA ITALIA SERIE C WINNER. GROUP PHASE: 5TH V 10TH, 6TH V 9TH, 7TH V 8TH, SINGLE MATCH AT THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB (IF LEVEL THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB), THEN THE WINNERS WITH THE 4TH. NATIONAL PHASE: TWO SEEDED ROUNDS OVER TWO LEGS, IF LEVEL THE SEEDED CLUB. FINAL FOUR: A FREE DRAW, TWO-LEGGED SEMI-FINALS AND FINAL, EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES.
COPPA ITALIA
THE REAL CUP HAS 44 CLUBS, WHO ENTER AT DIFFERENT ROUNDS BY DIVISION.

IN THE GAME IT STARTS FROM THE ROUND OF 16, WITH 16 CLUBS: THE ROUND OF 16 AND THE QUARTER-FINALS IN SINGLE MATCHES, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES, TWO-LEGGED SEMI-FINALS, A SINGLE-MATCH FINAL, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES. THE FIRST ROUND IS THE REAL DRAW.
COPPA ITALIA SERIE C
IN THE GAME IT STARTS WITH 17 CLUBS, KNOCKOUT. 15 OF THEM GO STRAIGHT TO THE SECOND ROUND.

THE FIRST ROUND, THE ROUND OF 16 AND THE QUARTER-FINALS ARE PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE SEMI-FINALS AND THE FINAL ARE PLAYED OVER TWO LEGS, WITH THE AWAY GOALS RULE. THE FIRST ROUND FOLLOWS THE REAL DRAW.
