LIGA NACIONAL
THE LIGA NACIONAL HAS 12 CLUBS AND TWO TOURNAMENTS, THE APERTURA 2026 AND THE CLAUSURA 2027, EACH OF 22 MATCHDAYS HOME AND AWAY, THE CLAUSURA STARTS AGAIN FROM ZERO POINTS, THE ANNUAL TABLE ADDS BOTH UP.

EACH TOURNAMENT HAS ITS OWN FINAL PHASE AND ITS OWN CHAMPION.

IN REALITY CLUBS GO DOWN TO THE PRIMERA DIVISION, IN THE GAME THE LIGA NACIONAL IS THE ONLY DIVISION: NOBODY GOES DOWN.

THE TOP 6 GO TO THE FINAL PHASE: QUARTER-FINALS 3RD V 6TH AND 4TH V 5TH, THEN SEMI-FINALS WHERE THE 1ST MEETS THE LOWEST PLACED QUALIFIER AND THE 2ND THE OTHER. QUARTER-FINALS AND SEMI-FINALS ARE HOME AND AWAY: LEVEL ON AGGREGATE, THE BETTER PLACED CLUB GOES THROUGH. THE FINAL IS HOME AND AWAY: LEVEL ON AGGREGATE, EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES.
