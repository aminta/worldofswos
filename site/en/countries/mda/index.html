The in-game FORMAT page

As the game shows it (FORMAT button of the competition).

SUPER LIGA
THE LEAGUE HAS 8 CLUBS WHO PLAY EACH OTHER THREE TIMES: 21 MATCHDAYS. A WIN IS WORTH 3 POINTS, A DRAW 1.

AFTER 21 MATCHDAYS THE TABLE SPLITS: CHAMPIONSHIP GROUP (1ST-6TH) PLAYS HOME AND AWAY, KEEPING ALL THE POINTS OF THE FIRST PHASE, THE CLUBS FROM 7TH TO 8TH STOP.

THE 7TH-8TH WOULD PLAY THE RELEGATION PLAY-OFFS AGAINST CLUBS FROM LIGA 1, WHICH IS NOT IN THE WORLD: HERE NOBODY GOES DOWN.

EUROPEAN PLACES BY THE ASSOCIATION UEFA RANKING (UEFA ACCESS LIST): THE 2026-27 TABLE FOR THE 2027-28 CUPS, REAL 2026 RANKING:
LIGHT BLUE 1ST: CHAMPIONS LEAGUE
DARK BLUE 2ND-3RD: CONFERENCE LEAGUE
THE CUP WINNER: EUROPA LEAGUE
FROM THE NEXT SEASON ON: SIMULATED RANKING
THE 2 EUROPEAN PERFORMANCE SPOTS AND THE TITLE HOLDERS REBALANCING ARE DECIDED AT THE SEASON END, NOT COLOURED IN ADVANCE
CUPA MOLDOVEI
THE REAL CUP HAS 32 CLUBS, WHO ENTER AT DIFFERENT ROUNDS BY DIVISION. IN THE GAME 8 OF THEM ARE LEFT, KNOCKOUT.

THE QUARTER-FINALS, THE SEMI-FINALS AND THE FINAL ARE PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE FIRST ROUND FOLLOWS THE REAL DRAW.
