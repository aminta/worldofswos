EL SALVADOR

EL SALVADOR: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

  • PRIMERA DIVISION

    12 clubs · double round robin, 22 matchdays · 1 down

  • SEGUNDA DIVISION OCCIDENTE / SEGUNDA DIVISION ORIENTE

    2 parallel groups of 12 + 12 (each club in the group of its area; promoted and relegated clubs join the group of their area) · double round robin, 22 matchdays · 1 down

    • SEGUNDA DIVISION FINAL promotion play-off: SEGUNDA DIVISION OCCIDENTE 1st + SEGUNDA DIVISION ORIENTE 1st; single match, neutral ground, extra time and penalties; the winner to PRIMERA DIVISION
  • TERCERA DIVISION OCCIDENTE / TERCERA DIVISION ORIENTE

    2 parallel groups of 9 + 10 (each club in the group of its area; promoted and relegated clubs join the group of their area) · double round robin, 18 matchdays · one club rests each matchday · 1 up · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

  • COPA EL SALVADORCup

    55 clubs · every club of the league, drawn by the game · 9 byes into the 2nd round · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties

  • Declared approximation

    the 2026-27 Tercera is not published yet: its clubs are the 2025-26 Tercera clubs not promoted, in two zones, with generated squads.

COPA EL SALVADOR
THE REAL CUP HAS 24 CLUBS, WHO ENTER AT DIFFERENT ROUNDS BY DIVISION. IN THE GAME 55 OF THEM ARE LEFT, KNOCKOUT. 9 OF THEM GO STRAIGHT TO THE SECOND ROUND.

THE FIRST ROUND, THE SECOND ROUND, THE ROUND OF 16, THE QUARTER-FINALS, THE SEMI-FINALS AND THE FINAL ARE PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL.
