Continental places (season 1)
  • CONCACAF CHAMPIONS CUP: 9 (1st, 1st-5th, next 2 best placed not yet qualified, cup winner) 4.0
  • MLS EASTERN CONFERENCE

    15 clubs · double round robin, 30 matchdays · one club rests each matchday · no promotion or relegation

  • MLS WESTERN CONFERENCE

    15 clubs · double round robin, 30 matchdays · one club rests each matchday · no promotion or relegation

  • U.S. OPEN CUPCup

The in-game FORMAT page

As the game shows it (FORMAT button of the competition).

MAJOR LEAGUE SOCCER
THE 30 CLUBS ARE SPLIT INTO TWO CONFERENCES OF 15, EAST AND WEST, AND PLAY 34 MATCHES IN ONE TABLE, THE CLUB WITH THE MOST POINTS WINS THE SUPPORTERS' SHIELD. NOBODY GOES DOWN.

THE TOP 9 OF EACH CONFERENCE GO TO THE MLS CUP PLAYOFFS: 8TH V 9TH IN A SINGLE MATCH (THE WILD CARD, IF LEVEL PENALTIES), THEN A BEST-OF-THREE FIRST ROUND, THEN THE CONFERENCE SEMI-FINALS AND FINALS AND THE MLS CUP, SINGLE MATCHES AT THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES.

TABLE COLOURS:
LIGHT BLUE 1ST-3RD: CONCACAF CHAMPIONS CUP
GREEN 1ST-9TH IN EACH GROUP: KNOCKOUT STAGE
THE CUP WINNER: CONCACAF CHAMPIONS CUP
MLS CUP PLAYOFFS
THE TOP 9 OF EACH CONFERENCE GO TO THE MLS CUP PLAYOFFS: 8TH V 9TH IN A SINGLE MATCH (THE WILD CARD, IF LEVEL PENALTIES), THEN A BEST-OF-THREE FIRST ROUND, THEN THE CONFERENCE SEMI-FINALS AND FINALS AND THE MLS CUP, SINGLE MATCHES AT THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES.
U.S. OPEN CUP
THE REAL CUP HAS 80 CLUBS, WHO ENTER AT DIFFERENT ROUNDS BY DIVISION. IN THE GAME 8 OF THEM ARE LEFT, KNOCKOUT.

THE QUARTER-FINALS, THE SEMI-FINALS AND THE FINAL ARE PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE FIRST ROUND FOLLOWS THE REAL DRAW.
