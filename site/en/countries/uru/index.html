URUGUAY: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

  • SEGUNDA DIVISION

    14 clubs · double round robin, 26 matchdays · 3 up · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

  • COPA AUF URUGUAYCup

    18 clubs · from the round of 32 · the clubs still in the real 2026 cup, all 2 first-round pairs as really drawn · 14 byes into the 2nd round · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties · rounds numbered as in reality
