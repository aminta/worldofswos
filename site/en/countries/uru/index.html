URUGUAY

URUGUAY: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

Continental places (season 1)
  • COPA LIBERTADORES: 4 (1st-4th)
  • COPA SUDAMERICANA: 4 (next 4 best placed not yet qualified)
  • CAMPEONATO URUGUAYO

    16 clubs · double round robin, 30 matchdays · 3 down

  • SEGUNDA DIVISION

    14 clubs · double round robin, 26 matchdays · 3 up · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

  • COPA AUF URUGUAYCup

    the real cup: 32 clubs (entering: schedule 24, 1st round 8) · in the game: 18 clubs · from the round of 32 · the clubs still in the real 2026 cup, all 2 first-round pairs as really drawn · 14 byes into the 2nd round · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties · rounds numbered as in reality

The in-game FORMAT page

As the game shows it (FORMAT button of the competition).

CAMPEONATO URUGUAYO
THE LEAGUE HAS 16 CLUBS AND THREE TOURNAMENTS: THE APERTURA AND THE CLAUSURA, 15 MATCHDAYS, EVERYBODY ONCE, ON THE REAL CALENDAR, AND THE INTERMEDIO BETWEEN THEM, EACH STARTS AGAIN FROM ZERO POINTS. THE ANNUAL TABLE ADDS UP ALL THREE.

THE TITLE: A SINGLE-MATCH SEMI-FINAL ON NEUTRAL GROUND BETWEEN THE APERTURA AND CLAUSURA CHAMPIONS, THEN A TWO-LEGGED FINAL AGAINST THE ANNUAL TABLE'S LEADER. IF THE ANNUAL LEADER WON THE APERTURA OR THE CLAUSURA, WINNING THE SEMI-FINAL IS ENOUGH, A CLUB WINNING BOTH THE APERTURA AND THE CLAUSURA IS CHAMPION WITHOUT PLAYING.

THE LAST 3 OF THE PROMEDIO (THE AVERAGE POINTS OF THE LAST 2 SEASONS) GO DOWN, IN THE GAME, THE LAST 3 OF THE END-OF-SEASON TABLE.

TABLE COLOURS:
LIGHT BLUE 1ST-4TH: COPA LIBERTADORES
GREEN 5TH-8TH: COPA SUDAMERICANA
GRAY 14TH-16TH: RELEGATED
SEGUNDA DIVISION
THE LEAGUE HAS 14 CLUBS WHO PLAY EACH OTHER HOME AND AWAY: 26 MATCHDAYS. A WIN IS WORTH 3 POINTS, A DRAW 1.

THE TOP 3 GO UP TO CAMPEONATO URUGUAYO. NOBODY GOES DOWN.

TABLE COLOURS:
LIGHT BLUE 1ST-3RD: PROMOTED
TORNEO APERTURA
THE TORNEO APERTURA OPENS THE SEASON: 15 MATCHDAYS, EVERYBODY ONCE, ON THE REAL CALENDAR. THE CLUB ON TOP WINS IT, AND ITS TABLE SETS THE INTERMEDIO'S TWO SERIES.
TORNEO INTERMEDIO
IN THE INTERMEDIO THE CLUBS SPLIT INTO TWO SERIES OF 8 BY THE APERTURA TABLE: SERIES A HAS THE 1ST, 4TH, 5TH, 8TH, 9TH, 12TH, 13TH AND 16TH, SERIES B THE OTHERS. EVERYBODY MEETS ONCE (7 MATCHDAYS) FROM ZERO POINTS, THEN THE TWO LEADERS PLAY A FINAL ON NEUTRAL GROUND, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES.
TORNEO CLAUSURA
THE TORNEO CLAUSURA CLOSES THE SEASON: BACK TO ZERO, 15 MATCHDAYS, EVERYBODY ONCE, ON THE REAL CALENDAR. THE CLUB ON TOP WINS IT, ITS CHAMPION GOES TO THE TITLE SEMI-FINAL.
CAMPEONATO URUGUAYO
THE LEAGUE HAS 16 CLUBS AND THREE TOURNAMENTS: THE APERTURA AND THE CLAUSURA, 15 MATCHDAYS, EVERYBODY ONCE, ON THE REAL CALENDAR, AND THE INTERMEDIO BETWEEN THEM, EACH STARTS AGAIN FROM ZERO POINTS. THE ANNUAL TABLE ADDS UP ALL THREE.

THE TITLE: A SINGLE-MATCH SEMI-FINAL ON NEUTRAL GROUND BETWEEN THE APERTURA AND CLAUSURA CHAMPIONS, THEN A TWO-LEGGED FINAL AGAINST THE ANNUAL TABLE'S LEADER. IF THE ANNUAL LEADER WON THE APERTURA OR THE CLAUSURA, WINNING THE SEMI-FINAL IS ENOUGH, A CLUB WINNING BOTH THE APERTURA AND THE CLAUSURA IS CHAMPION WITHOUT PLAYING.

THE LAST 3 OF THE PROMEDIO (THE AVERAGE POINTS OF THE LAST 2 SEASONS) GO DOWN, IN THE GAME, THE LAST 3 OF THE END-OF-SEASON TABLE.
COPA AUF URUGUAY
THE REAL CUP HAS 32 CLUBS, WHO ENTER AT DIFFERENT ROUNDS BY DIVISION. IN THE GAME 18 OF THEM ARE LEFT, KNOCKOUT. 14 OF THEM GO STRAIGHT TO THE SECOND ROUND.

THE FIRST ROUND, THE ROUND OF 16, THE QUARTER-FINALS, THE SEMI-FINALS AND THE FINAL ARE PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE FIRST ROUND FOLLOWS THE REAL DRAW.

FROM THE SECOND SEASON THE CLUBS FOLLOW PROMOTIONS AND RELEGATIONS: EACH DIVISION SENDS AS MANY CLUBS AS IN THE REAL CUP. FIRST THOSE RELEGATED FROM THE DIVISION ABOVE, THEN THOSE THAT STAYED, THEN THE PROMOTED ONES, EACH BY LAST SEASON'S PLACE, YOUR CLUB ALWAYS TAKES PART WHEN ITS DIVISION DOES.
