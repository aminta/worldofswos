ISRAEL

ISRAEL: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

  • ISRAELI PREMIER LEAGUE

    14 clubs · double round robin (26 matchdays) · after 26 matchdays: 1-6 double round robin, 7-14 single round robin · 36 matchdays · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

  • ISRAEL STATE CUPCup

    14 clubs · every club of the league, drawn by the game · 2 byes into the 2nd round · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties

  • Europe

    season 1: the real 2026-27 league-phase clubs (Europa League H. Beer-Sheva) · from season 2: Europa League 1, Conference League 1 by the simulated association coefficients
