ARGENTINA

ARGENTINA: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

Continental places (season 1)
  • COPA LIBERTADORES: 6 (1st-3rd, Apertura champion, Clausura champion, cup winner) 4.0
  • COPA SUDAMERICANA: 6 (next 6 best placed not yet qualified) 4.0
  • LIGA PROFESIONAL ZONA A

    15 clubs · double round robin, 30 matchdays · one club rests each matchday · no promotion or relegation

  • LIGA PROFESIONAL ZONA B

    15 clubs · double round robin, 30 matchdays · one club rests each matchday · no promotion or relegation

  • PRIMERA NACIONAL ZONA A / PRIMERA NACIONAL ZONA B

    2 parallel groups of 18 + 18 (each club in the group of its area; promoted and relegated clubs join the group of their area) · double round robin, 34 matchdays · no promotion or relegation

  • COPA ARGENTINACup

    8 clubs · from the quarter-finals · the clubs still in the real 2026 cup, all 4 first-round pairs as really drawn · single matches (a draw: penalties) · final: single match (a draw: penalties)

The in-game FORMAT page

As the game shows it (FORMAT button of the competition).

LIGA PROFESIONAL
THE LEAGUE IS PLAYED AS TWO TOURNAMENTS, THE APERTURA AND THE CLAUSURA. IN EACH, THE 30 CLUBS ARE SPLIT INTO TWO ZONES OF 15: EVERY CLUB MEETS THE OTHER 14 OF ITS ZONE ONCE, ITS TRADITIONAL RIVAL FROM THE OTHER ZONE AND ONE DRAWN OPPONENT (16 MATCHDAYS). THE TOP 8 OF EACH ZONE GO INTO THE ROUND OF 16, CROSSED BETWEEN THE ZONES (1ST V 8TH AND SO ON), THEN QUARTER-FINALS, SEMI-FINALS AND A FINAL ON NEUTRAL GROUND: THE WINNER IS THE TOURNAMENT'S CHAMPION.

THE ANNUAL TABLE ADDS UP BOTH TOURNAMENTS AND GIVES THE CAMPEON DE LIGA TITLE AND THE LIBERTADORES AND SUDAMERICANA PLACES. RELEGATED: THE CLUB WITH THE WORST PROMEDIO (THE AVERAGE POINTS OF THE LAST 3 SEASONS) AND THE LAST OF THE ANNUAL TABLE.

TABLE COLOURS:
LIGHT BLUE 1ST-5TH: COPA LIBERTADORES
GREEN 6TH-11TH: COPA SUDAMERICANA
GREEN 1ST-8TH IN EACH GROUP: KNOCKOUT STAGE
THE CUP WINNER: COPA LIBERTADORES
PRIMERA NACIONAL ZONA A
THE GROUP HAS 18 CLUBS WHO PLAY EACH OTHER HOME AND AWAY: 34 MATCHDAYS. A WIN IS WORTH 3 POINTS, A DRAW 1.

THE 1ST PLAYS THE PROMOTION PLAY-OFFS: A SINGLE MATCH, ON NEUTRAL GROUND, IF LEVEL, EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES. THE WINNER GOES UP TO LIGA PROFESIONAL.

THE CLUBS FROM 2ND TO 8TH PLAY THE PROMOTION PLAY-OFFS: ROUND 1: A SINGLE MATCH, AT THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB, IF LEVEL, THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB GOES THROUGH, ROUND 2: TWO LEGS, AT THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB, IF LEVEL, THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB GOES THROUGH, ROUND 3: TWO LEGS, AT THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB, IF LEVEL, THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB GOES THROUGH, ROUND 4: TWO LEGS, AT THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB, IF LEVEL, THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB GOES THROUGH. THE WINNER GOES UP TO LIGA PROFESIONAL.

PRIMERA NACIONAL ZONA B

THE CLUBS FROM 2ND TO 8TH PLAY THE PROMOTION PLAY-OFFS: ROUND 1: A SINGLE MATCH, AT THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB, IF LEVEL, THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB GOES THROUGH, ROUND 2: TWO LEGS, AT THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB, IF LEVEL, THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB GOES THROUGH, ROUND 3: TWO LEGS, AT THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB, IF LEVEL, THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB GOES THROUGH, ROUND 4: TWO LEGS, AT THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB, IF LEVEL, THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB GOES THROUGH. PRIMERA NACIONAL FINAL

REDUCIDO

ROUND 2: TWO LEGS, LEVEL: THE BETTER PLACED GOES THROUGH. ROUND 3: TWO LEGS, LEVEL: THE BETTER PLACED GOES THROUGH. ROUND 4: TWO LEGS, LEVEL: THE BETTER PLACED GOES THROUGH. COPA ARGENTINA
THE REAL CUP HAS 64 CLUBS, WHO ENTER AT DIFFERENT ROUNDS BY DIVISION. IN THE GAME 8 OF THEM ARE LEFT, KNOCKOUT.

THE QUARTER-FINALS, THE SEMI-FINALS AND THE FINAL ARE PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE FIRST ROUND FOLLOWS THE REAL DRAW.
