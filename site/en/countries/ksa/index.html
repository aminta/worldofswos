SAUDI PRO LEAGUE
THE LEAGUE HAS 18 CLUBS WHO PLAY EACH OTHER HOME AND AWAY: 34 MATCHDAYS. A WIN IS WORTH 3 POINTS, A DRAW 1.

THE 16TH-18TH WOULD GO DOWN TO FIRST DIVISION, WHICH IS NOT IN THE WORLD: HERE NOBODY GOES DOWN.

TABLE COLOURS:
LIGHT BLUE 1ST-5TH: AFC CHAMPIONS LEAGUE ELITE
GREEN 6TH: AFC CHAMPIONS LEAGUE TWO
KING'S CUP
IN THE GAME IT STARTS WITH 11 CLUBS, KNOCKOUT. 5 OF THEM GO STRAIGHT TO THE SECOND ROUND.

THE FIRST ROUND, THE QUARTER-FINALS, THE SEMI-FINALS AND THE FINAL ARE PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE FIRST ROUND FOLLOWS THE REAL DRAW.
