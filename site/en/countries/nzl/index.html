  • SOUTHERN LEAGUE

    12 clubs · from the round of 16 · the clubs still in the real 2026 cup, all 4 first-round pairs as really drawn · 4 byes into the 2nd round · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties · rounds numbered as in reality

CHATHAM CUP
THE REAL CUP HAS 101 CLUBS, WHO ENTER AT DIFFERENT ROUNDS BY DIVISION. IN THE GAME 12 OF THEM ARE LEFT, KNOCKOUT. 4 OF THEM GO STRAIGHT TO THE SECOND ROUND.

THE FIRST ROUND, THE QUARTER-FINALS, THE SEMI-FINALS AND THE FINAL ARE PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE FIRST ROUND FOLLOWS THE REAL DRAW.
