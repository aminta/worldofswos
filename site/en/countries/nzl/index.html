NEW ZEALAND

NEW ZEALAND: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

  • NORTHERN LEAGUE

    12 clubs · double round robin, 22 matchdays · no promotion or relegation

  • CENTRAL LEAGUE

    10 clubs · double round robin, 18 matchdays · no promotion or relegation

  • SOUTHERN LEAGUE

    10 clubs · double round robin, 18 matchdays · no promotion or relegation

  • CHATHAM CUPCup

    12 clubs · from the round of 16 · the clubs still in the real 2026 cup, all 4 first-round pairs as really drawn · 4 byes into the 2nd round · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties · rounds numbered as in reality

  • Declared approximation

    for now the season is the three regional leagues only (closed) · the National League Championship (their top clubs + the A-League reserves, Sep-Dec) comes as a second competition of the season

  • Declared approximation

    the A-League reserves play no Chatham Cup (no regulation found, Davide's choice)
