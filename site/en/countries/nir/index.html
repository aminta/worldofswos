N. IRELAND

N. IRELAND: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

No direct continental place in season 1.

  • NIFL PREMIERSHIP

    12 clubs · triple round robin (33 matchdays) · after 33 matchdays: 1-6 single round robin, 7-12 single round robin · home order: the club that was away in their 3rd meeting · 38 matchdays · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

    • EUROPA CONFERENCE PLAY-OFF European play-off: NIFL PREMIERSHIP 4th-7th; 3 rounds of single match, the better placed at home, extra time and penalties
  • IRISH CUPCup

    12 clubs · every club of the league, drawn by the game · 4 byes into the 2nd round · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties · rounds numbered as in reality

  • IRISH LEAGUE CUPLeague cup

    12 clubs · from the round of 16 · the clubs still in the real 2026-27 cup, 2 of the 4 first-round pairs as really drawn · 4 byes into the 2nd round · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties

NIFL PREMIERSHIP
THE LEAGUE HAS 12 CLUBS WHO PLAY EACH OTHER THREE TIMES: 33 MATCHDAYS. A WIN IS WORTH 3 POINTS, A DRAW 1.

AFTER 33 MATCHDAYS THE TABLE SPLITS: CHAMPIONSHIP GROUP (1ST-6TH) PLAYS ONCE MORE, KEEPING ALL THE POINTS OF THE FIRST PHASE, RELEGATION GROUP (7TH-12TH) PLAYS ONCE MORE, KEEPING ALL THE POINTS OF THE FIRST PHASE.

THE 12TH WOULD GO DOWN TO CHAMPIONSHIP, WHICH IS NOT IN THE WORLD, THE 11TH WOULD PLAY THE RELEGATION PLAY-OFF: HERE NOBODY GOES DOWN.

THE CLUBS FROM 4TH TO 7TH PLAY THE EUROPEAN PLAY-OFFS (EUROPEAN PLAY-OFF): A SINGLE MATCH, AT THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB, IF LEVEL, EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES.

IRISH CUP
THE REAL CUP HAS 32 CLUBS, WHO ENTER AT DIFFERENT ROUNDS BY DIVISION. IN THE GAME 12 OF THEM ARE LEFT, KNOCKOUT. 4 OF THEM GO STRAIGHT TO THE SECOND ROUND.

THE FIRST ROUND, THE QUARTER-FINALS, THE SEMI-FINALS AND THE FINAL ARE PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL.
IRISH LEAGUE CUP
IN THE GAME IT STARTS WITH 12 CLUBS, KNOCKOUT. 4 OF THEM GO STRAIGHT TO THE SECOND ROUND.

THE FIRST ROUND, THE QUARTER-FINALS, THE SEMI-FINALS AND THE FINAL ARE PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE FIRST ROUND FOLLOWS THE REAL DRAW.
