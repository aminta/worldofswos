ENGLAND

ENGLAND: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

  • PREMIER LEAGUE

    20 clubs · double round robin, 38 matchdays · 3 down

  • EFL CHAMPIONSHIP

    24 clubs · double round robin, 46 matchdays · 2 up · 3 down

    • CHAMPIONSHIP PLAY-OFFS promotion play-off: EFL CHAMPIONSHIP 3rd-8th; round 1: single match, home order as in the real schedule, extra time and penalties; round 2: two legs, the better placed at home (2nd leg), extra time and penalties; round 3: single match, neutral ground, extra time and penalties; the winner to PREMIER LEAGUE
  • EFL LEAGUE ONE

    24 clubs · double round robin, 46 matchdays · 2 up · 4 down

    • LEAGUE ONE PLAY-OFFS promotion play-off: EFL LEAGUE ONE 3rd-6th; round 1: two legs, home order as in the real schedule, extra time and penalties; round 2: single match, neutral ground, extra time and penalties; the winner to EFL CHAMPIONSHIP
  • EFL LEAGUE TWO

    24 clubs · double round robin, 46 matchdays · 3 up · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

    • LEAGUE TWO PLAY-OFFS promotion play-off: EFL LEAGUE TWO 4th-7th; round 1: two legs, home order as in the real schedule, extra time and penalties; round 2: single match, neutral ground, extra time and penalties; the winner to EFL LEAGUE ONE
  • FA CUPCup

    64 clubs · every club of the league, drawn by the game · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties · rounds numbered as in reality

  • EFL CUPLeague cup

    16 clubs · from the round of 16 · the clubs still in the real 2026-27 cup, all 8 first-round pairs as really drawn · rounds 1-2: single matches (a draw: penalties) · round 3: two legs (aggregate, then penalties, no extra time) · final: single match, extra time and penalties · rounds numbered as in reality

  • Europe

    season 1: the real 2026-27 league-phase clubs (Champions League Man City, Liverpool, Man Utd, Aston Villa, Arsenal; Europa League Sunderland, Bournemouth, Crystal Palace; Conference League Brighton) · from season 2: Champions League 5, Europa League 2, Conference League 1 by the simulated association coefficients
