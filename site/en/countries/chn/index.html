As the game shows it (FORMAT button of the competition).

CHINESE SUPER LEAGUE
THE LEAGUE HAS 16 CLUBS WHO PLAY EACH OTHER HOME AND AWAY: 30 MATCHDAYS. A WIN IS WORTH 3 POINTS, A DRAW 1.

THE 15TH-16TH WOULD GO DOWN TO CHINA LEAGUE ONE, WHICH IS NOT IN THE WORLD: HERE NOBODY GOES DOWN.

TABLE COLOURS:
LIGHT BLUE 1ST: AFC CHAMPIONS LEAGUE ELITE
GREEN 2ND: AFC CHAMPIONS LEAGUE TWO
THE CUP WINNER: AFC CHAMPIONS LEAGUE ELITE
CHINESE FA CUP
THE REAL CUP HAS 82 CLUBS, WHO ENTER AT DIFFERENT ROUNDS BY DIVISION. IN THE GAME 4 OF THEM ARE LEFT, KNOCKOUT.

THE SEMI-FINALS AND THE FINAL ARE PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE FIRST ROUND FOLLOWS THE REAL DRAW.
