The in-game FORMAT page

As the game shows it (FORMAT button of the competition).

CANADIAN PREMIER LEAGUE
THE LEAGUE HAS 8 CLUBS WHO PLAY EACH OTHER FOUR TIMES: 28 MATCHDAYS. A WIN IS WORTH 3 POINTS, A DRAW 1.

NOBODY GOES DOWN.

THE CLUBS FROM 1ST TO 4TH PLAY THE TITLE PLAY-OFFS (CPL PLAYOFFS): ROUND 1: TWO LEGS, AT THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB, IF LEVEL, EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES, ROUND 2: A SINGLE MATCH, AT THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB, IF LEVEL, EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES.

TABLE COLOURS:
LIGHT BLUE 1ST: CONCACAF CHAMPIONS CUP
GREEN 2ND-4TH: TITLE PLAY-OFF
THE CUP WINNER: CONCACAF CHAMPIONS CUP
IN REALITY: THE CHAMPION OF A FINAL / THE PLAY-OFFS, THEN THE ANNUAL TABLE, HERE THE GAME S TABLE
CPL PLAYOFFS
THE TOP 4 OF THE CANADIAN PREMIER LEAGUE PLAY THE PLAYOFFS: TWO-LEGGED SEMI-FINALS, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES, AND A SINGLE-MATCH FINAL AT THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES. THE WINNER IS THE CANADIAN CHAMPION.
CANADIAN CHAMPIONSHIP
THE REAL CUP HAS 15 CLUBS, WHO ENTER AT DIFFERENT ROUNDS BY DIVISION. IN THE GAME 8 OF THEM ARE LEFT, KNOCKOUT.

THE QUARTER-FINALS, THE SEMI-FINALS AND THE FINAL ARE PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE FIRST ROUND FOLLOWS THE REAL DRAW.
