FRANCE

FRANCE: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

Continental places (season 1)
  • UEFA Champions League: 3 (1st-3rd)
  • UEFA Europa League: 2 (cup winner, 4th)
  • UEFA Conference League: 1 (next by league place)
  • LIGUE 1

    18 clubs · double round robin, 34 matchdays · 2 down

    • BARRAGES LIGUE 1 promotion play-off: LIGUE 2 3rd-5th + LIGUE 1 16th; round 1: single match, home order as in the real schedule, extra time and penalties; round 2: single match, home order as in the real schedule, extra time and penalties; round 3: two legs, home order as in the real schedule, extra time and penalties; the winner to LIGUE 1
  • LIGUE 2

    18 clubs · double round robin, 34 matchdays · 2 up · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

  • COUPE DE FRANCECup

    36 clubs · from the round of 64 · the clubs still in the real 2026-27 cup, 0 of the 4 first-round pairs as really drawn · 28 byes into the 2nd round · single matches (a draw: penalties) · final: single match, extra time and penalties

  • Europe

    season 1: the real 2026-27 league-phase clubs (Champions League Lille, Lens, Paris; Europa League Lyon, Marseille, Rennes; Conference League Monaco) · from season 2: Champions League 3, Europa League 2, Conference League 1 by the simulated association coefficients

The in-game FORMAT page

As the game shows it (FORMAT button of the competition).

LIGUE 1
THE LEAGUE HAS 18 CLUBS WHO PLAY EACH OTHER HOME AND AWAY: 34 MATCHDAYS. A WIN IS WORTH 3 POINTS, A DRAW 1.

THE BOTTOM 2 GO DOWN TO LIGUE 2.

THE 16TH PLAYS THE PROMOTION PLAY-OFFS: ROUND 1: A SINGLE MATCH, IF LEVEL, EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES, ROUND 2: A SINGLE MATCH, IF LEVEL, EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES, ROUND 3: TWO LEGS, IF LEVEL, EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES.

LIGUE 2
THE LEAGUE HAS 18 CLUBS WHO PLAY EACH OTHER HOME AND AWAY: 34 MATCHDAYS. A WIN IS WORTH 3 POINTS, A DRAW 1.

THE TOP 2 GO UP TO LIGUE 1. THE 17TH-18TH WOULD GO DOWN TO NATIONAL, WHICH IS NOT IN THE WORLD, THE 16TH WOULD PLAY THE RELEGATION PLAY-OFF: HERE NOBODY GOES DOWN.

THE CLUBS FROM 3RD TO 5TH PLAY THE PROMOTION PLAY-OFFS: ROUND 1: A SINGLE MATCH, IF LEVEL, EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES, ROUND 2: A SINGLE MATCH, IF LEVEL, EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES, ROUND 3: TWO LEGS, IF LEVEL, EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES. THE WINNER GOES UP TO LIGUE 1.

TABLE COLOURS:
LIGHT BLUE 1ST-2ND: PROMOTED
GREEN 3RD-5TH: PROMOTION PLAY-OFF
LIGUE 1 PLAY-OFF
THE 16TH OF LIGUE 1 AND THE CLUBS FROM 3RD TO 5TH OF LIGUE 2 PLAY THE PROMOTION PLAY-OFFS. ROUND 1: SINGLE MATCH, EXTRA TIME, PENALTIES. ROUND 2: SINGLE MATCH, EXTRA TIME, PENALTIES. ROUND 3: TWO LEGS, AWAY GOALS. THE WINNER TO LIGUE 1.
COUPE DE FRANCE
THE REAL CUP HAS 262 CLUBS, WHO ENTER AT DIFFERENT ROUNDS BY DIVISION.

A KNOCKOUT CUP OPEN TO EVERY CLUB, SINGLE MATCHES AT THE LOWER-DIVISION CLUB. IF LEVEL, STRAIGHT TO PENALTIES, NO EXTRA TIME, EXCEPT IN THE FINAL. THE FINAL AT THE STADE DE FRANCE.
