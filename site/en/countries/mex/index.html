As the game shows it (FORMAT button of the competition).

LIGA MX
THE LIGA MX HAS 18 CLUBS AND IS PLAYED AS TWO TOURNAMENTS ON THE REAL CALENDAR: THE APERTURA (MATCHDAYS 1-17) AND THE CLAUSURA (MATCHDAYS 18-34). IN EACH, THE CLUBS MEET ONCE, THE CLAUSURA STARTS AGAIN FROM ZERO POINTS. A WIN IS WORTH 3 POINTS, A DRAW 1.

AFTER THE TOURNAMENT THE TOP 8 PLAY THE LIGUILLA: QUARTER-FINALS 1ST V 8TH, 2ND V 7TH, 3RD V 6TH AND 4TH V 5TH, THEN SEMI-FINALS WITH THE BEST PLACED CLUB LEFT AGAINST THE WORST PLACED ONE. QUARTER-FINALS AND SEMI-FINALS ARE OVER TWO LEGS, AND IF LEVEL THE CLUB PLACED HIGHER IN THE TOURNAMENT GOES THROUGH. THE FINAL IS OVER TWO LEGS, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL, ITS WINNER IS THE TOURNAMENT'S CHAMPION.

AT THE END OF THE SEASON THE TWO CHAMPIONS PLAY THE CAMPEON DE CAMPEONES, A SINGLE MATCH AT A NEUTRAL GROUND, WITH PENALTIES IF LEVEL, A CLUB WINNING BOTH TOURNAMENTS TAKES IT WITHOUT PLAYING. NOBODY GOES DOWN.

IN REALITY THE CONCACAF CHAMPIONS CUP PLACES GO TO THE TWO TOURNAMENTS' CHAMPIONS AND RUNNERS-UP AND TO THE BEST OF THE ANNUAL TABLE, IN THE GAME THEY GO TO THE BEST OF THE FINAL TABLE.

CAMPEON DE CAMPEONES
THE CAMPEON DE CAMPEONES PUTS THE APERTURA AND CLAUSURA CHAMPIONS AGAINST EACH OTHER. IN THE LIGA MX IT IS A SINGLE MATCH AT A NEUTRAL GROUND, WITH PENALTIES IF LEVEL, IN THE LIGA DE EXPANSION MX OVER TWO LEGS, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL. A CLUB WINNING BOTH TOURNAMENTS TAKES IT WITHOUT PLAYING.
