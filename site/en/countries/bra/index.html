BRAZIL

BRAZIL: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

Continental places (season 1)
  • COPA LIBERTADORES: 7 (1st-6th, cup winner)
  • COPA SUDAMERICANA: 6 (next 6 best placed not yet qualified)
  • BRASILEIRAO SERIE A

    20 clubs · double round robin, 38 matchdays · 4 down

  • BRASILEIRAO SERIE B

    20 clubs · double round robin, 38 matchdays · 2 up · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

    • SERIE B PROMOTION GR. A promotion play-off: BRASILEIRAO SERIE B 3rd, 6th; two legs, home order as in the real schedule, level: the better placed goes through; the winner to BRASILEIRAO SERIE A
    • SERIE B PROMOTION GR. B promotion play-off: BRASILEIRAO SERIE B 4th-5th; two legs, home order as in the real schedule, level: the better placed goes through; the winner to BRASILEIRAO SERIE A
  • COPA DO BRASILCup

    the real cup: 126 clubs (entering: 1st round 28, 2nd round 74, 3rd round 4, 5th round 20) · in the game: 8 clubs · from the quarter-finals · the clubs still in the real 2026 cup, all 4 first-round pairs as really drawn · two legs (aggregate, then penalties, no extra time) · final: single match (a draw: penalties)

The in-game FORMAT page

As the game shows it (FORMAT button of the competition).

BRASILEIRAO SERIE A
THE LEAGUE HAS 20 CLUBS WHO PLAY EACH OTHER HOME AND AWAY: 38 MATCHDAYS. A WIN IS WORTH 3 POINTS, A DRAW 1.

THE BOTTOM 4 GO DOWN TO BRASILEIRAO SERIE B.

TABLE COLOURS:
LIGHT BLUE 1ST-6TH: COPA LIBERTADORES
GREEN 7TH-12TH: COPA SUDAMERICANA
GRAY 17TH-20TH: RELEGATED
THE CUP WINNER: COPA LIBERTADORES
BRASILEIRAO SERIE B
THE LEAGUE HAS 20 CLUBS WHO PLAY EACH OTHER HOME AND AWAY: 38 MATCHDAYS. A WIN IS WORTH 3 POINTS, A DRAW 1.

THE TOP 2 GO UP TO BRASILEIRAO SERIE A. THE 17TH-20TH WOULD GO DOWN TO SERIE C, WHICH IS NOT IN THE WORLD: HERE NOBODY GOES DOWN.

THE CLUBS 3RD AND 6TH PLAY THE PROMOTION PLAY-OFFS: TWO LEGS, IF LEVEL, THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB GOES THROUGH. THE WINNER GOES UP TO BRASILEIRAO SERIE A.

THE CLUBS FROM 4TH TO 5TH PLAY THE PROMOTION PLAY-OFFS: TWO LEGS, IF LEVEL, THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB GOES THROUGH. THE WINNER GOES UP TO BRASILEIRAO SERIE A.

TABLE COLOURS:
LIGHT BLUE 1ST-2ND: PROMOTED
GREEN 3RD-6TH: PROMOTION PLAY-OFF
SERIE B PROMOTION GR. A
THE 3RD AND 6TH PLAY THE PROMOTION PLAY-OFFS. TWO LEGS, LEVEL: THE BETTER PLACED GOES THROUGH. THE WINNER TO BRASILEIRAO SERIE A.
SERIE B PROMOTION GR. B
THE CLUBS FROM 4TH TO 5TH PLAY THE PROMOTION PLAY-OFFS. TWO LEGS, LEVEL: THE BETTER PLACED GOES THROUGH. THE WINNER TO BRASILEIRAO SERIE A.
COPA DO BRASIL
THE REAL CUP HAS 126 CLUBS, WHO ENTER AT DIFFERENT ROUNDS BY DIVISION. IN THE GAME 8 OF THEM ARE LEFT, KNOCKOUT.

THE QUARTER-FINALS AND THE SEMI-FINALS ARE PLAYED OVER TWO LEGS, WITH PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE FINAL IS PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE FIRST ROUND FOLLOWS THE REAL DRAW.

FROM THE SECOND SEASON THE CLUBS FOLLOW PROMOTIONS AND RELEGATIONS: EACH DIVISION SENDS AS MANY CLUBS AS IN THE REAL CUP. FIRST THOSE RELEGATED FROM THE DIVISION ABOVE, THEN THOSE THAT STAYED, THEN THE PROMOTED ONES, EACH BY LAST SEASON'S PLACE, YOUR CLUB ALWAYS TAKES PART WHEN ITS DIVISION DOES.
