BRAZIL

BRAZIL: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

  • CAMPEONATO BRASILEIRO SERIE A

    20 clubs · double round robin, 38 matchdays · 4 down

  • CAMPEONATO BRASILEIRO SERIE B

    20 clubs · double round robin, 38 matchdays · 2 up · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

    • ACESSO SERIE B GRUPO A promotion play-off: CAMPEONATO BRASILEIRO SERIE B 3rd, 6th; two legs, home order as in the real schedule, level: the better placed goes through; the winner to CAMPEONATO BRASILEIRO SERIE A
    • ACESSO SERIE B GRUPO B promotion play-off: CAMPEONATO BRASILEIRO SERIE B 4th-5th; two legs, home order as in the real schedule, level: the better placed goes through; the winner to CAMPEONATO BRASILEIRO SERIE A
  • COPA DO BRASILCup

    8 clubs · from the quarter-finals · the clubs still in the real 2026 cup, all 4 first-round pairs as really drawn · two legs (aggregate, then penalties, no extra time) · final: single match (a draw: penalties)
