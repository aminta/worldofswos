ECUADOR

ECUADOR: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

  • LIGAPRO SERIE A

    16 clubs · double round robin (30 matchdays) · after 30 matchdays: 1-6 double round robin, 7-10 double round robin, 11-16 double round robin · 40 matchdays · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

  • COPA ECUADORCup

    9 clubs · from the round of 16 · the clubs still in the real 2026 cup, the only first-round pair as really drawn · 7 byes into the 2nd round · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties
