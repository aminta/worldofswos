JAPAN

JAPAN: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

  • J1 LEAGUE

    20 clubs · double round robin, 38 matchdays · 3 down

  • J2 LEAGUE

    20 clubs · double round robin, 38 matchdays · 2 up · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

    • J1 PROMOTION PLAY-OFFS promotion play-off: J2 LEAGUE 3rd-6th; 2 rounds of single match, the better placed at home, level: the better placed goes through; the winner to J1 LEAGUE
  • EMPEROR'S CUPCup

    16 clubs · from the round of 16 · the clubs still in the real 2026-27 cup, 0 of the 8 first-round pairs as really drawn · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties

  • J.LEAGUE CUPLeague cup

    12 clubs · from the round of 16 · the clubs still in the real 2026-27 cup, all 4 first-round pairs as really drawn · 4 byes into the 2nd round · round 1: single matches, extra time and penalties · rounds 2-3: two legs (aggregate, then extra time and penalties) · final: single match, extra time and penalties
