ROMANIA: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

  • SUPERLIGA

    16 clubs · double round robin (30 matchdays) · after 30 matchdays: 1-6 double round robin, points halved (rounded up), 7-16 single round robin, points halved (rounded up) · 40 matchdays · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

    • BARAJ CONFERENCE LEAGUE European play-off: SUPERLIGA 7th-8th; 2 rounds of single match, home order as in the real schedule, extra time and penalties
  • CUPA ROMANIEICup

    14 clubs · from the round of 16 · the clubs still in the real 2026-27 cup, 0 of the 6 first-round pairs as really drawn · 2 byes into the 2nd round · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties
