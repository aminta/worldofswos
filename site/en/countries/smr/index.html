SAN MARINO

SAN MARINO: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

  • CAMPIONATO SAMMARINESE

    16 clubs · double round robin, 30 matchdays · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

    • PLAY-OFF CONFERENCE LEAGUE European play-off: CAMPIONATO SAMMARINESE 2nd-11th; round 1: single match, home order as in the real schedule, level: the better placed goes through; round 2: two legs, the better placed at home (2nd leg), level: the better placed goes through; round 3: two legs, the better placed at home (2nd leg), level: the better placed goes through; round 4: single match, the better placed at home, extra time and penalties
  • COPPA TITANOCup

    16 clubs · from the round of 16 · the clubs still in the real 2026-27 cup, 7 of the 8 first-round pairs as really drawn · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties

  • Europe

As the game shows it (FORMAT button of the competition).

CAMPIONATO SAMMARINESE
THE LEAGUE HAS 16 CLUBS WHO PLAY EACH OTHER HOME AND AWAY: 30 MATCHDAYS. A WIN IS WORTH 3 POINTS, A DRAW 1.

NOBODY GOES DOWN.

THE CLUBS FROM 2ND TO 11TH PLAY THE EUROPEAN PLAY-OFFS (EUROPEAN PLAY-OFF): ROUND 1: A SINGLE MATCH, IF LEVEL, THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB GOES THROUGH, ROUND 2: TWO LEGS, AT THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB, IF LEVEL, THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB GOES THROUGH, ROUND 3: TWO LEGS, AT THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB, IF LEVEL, THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB GOES THROUGH, ROUND 4: A SINGLE MATCH, AT THE BETTER-PLACED CLUB, IF LEVEL, EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES.

EUROPEAN PLAY-OFF
THE CLUBS FROM 2ND TO 11TH PLAY THE EUROPEAN PLAY-OFFS. ROUND 1: SINGLE MATCH, LEVEL: THE BETTER PLACED GOES THROUGH. ROUND 2: TWO LEGS, LEVEL: THE BETTER PLACED GOES THROUGH. ROUND 3: TWO LEGS, LEVEL: THE BETTER PLACED GOES THROUGH. ROUND 4: SINGLE MATCH, EXTRA TIME, PENALTIES.
COPPA TITANO
IN THE GAME IT STARTS WITH 16 CLUBS, KNOCKOUT.

THE ROUND OF 16, THE QUARTER-FINALS, THE SEMI-FINALS AND THE FINAL ARE PLAYED AS ONE MATCH, WITH EXTRA TIME AND PENALTIES IF LEVEL. THE FIRST ROUND FOLLOWS THE REAL DRAW.
