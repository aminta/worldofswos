FINLAND

FINLAND: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

  • VEIKKAUSLIIGA

    12 clubs · double round robin (22 matchdays) · after 22 matchdays: 1-6 single round robin, 7-12 single round robin · 27 matchdays · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

  • SUOMEN CUPCup

    9 clubs · from the round of 16 · the clubs still in the real 2026 cup, the only first-round pair as really drawn · 7 byes into the 2nd round · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties · rounds numbered as in reality

  • LIIGACUPLeague cup

    12 clubs · every club of the league, drawn by the game · 4 byes into the 2nd round · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties

  • Europe

    season 1: the real 2026-27 league-phase clubs (Conference League KuPS Kuopio) · from season 2: Conference League 1 by the simulated association coefficients
