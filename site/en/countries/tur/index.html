TURKEY: every playable competition in the World 2026-27, with its format.

  • SUPER LIG

    18 clubs · double round robin, 34 matchdays · 3 down

  • TFF 1. LIG

    20 clubs · double round robin, 38 matchdays · 2 up · the lowest division of the world: nobody goes down

    • TFF 1. LIG PLAY-OFF promotion play-off: TFF 1. LIG 3rd-7th; round 1: single match, home order as in the real schedule, extra time and penalties; round 2: two legs, the better placed at home (2nd leg), extra time and penalties; round 3: single match, neutral ground, extra time and penalties; the winner to SUPER LIG
  • TURKIYE KUPASICup

    38 clubs · from the round of 64 · the clubs still in the real 2026-27 cup, 0 of the 6 first-round pairs as really drawn · 26 byes into the 2nd round · single matches, extra time and penalties · final: single match, extra time and penalties
