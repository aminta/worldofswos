What the mod is

SWOS 96/97 Mod patches your own copy of Sensible World of Soccer 96/97, the game we have played for thirty years, in all four of its languages: Italian, English, French and German.

Nothing of the game you love is lost: same pitch, same players running, same menus. What changes is the world around them. Version 4.0 brings a new career, World 2026-27: the 2026-27 season of 91 countries, every league, cup and play-off rebuilt exactly as its federation wrote it.

The highlights of 4.0

Every league in its real format

22 leagues split mid-season as their regulations say, promotion and relegation play-offs in 17 countries, European play-offs, Argentina's Apertura and Clausura with relegation by promedio, the MLS with its two conferences and the MLS Cup Playoffs.

European cups as UEFA plays them

The league phase of 36 with 8 matches (6 in the Conference League), one table with all ten UEFA tie-breakers, the knockout play-offs and the seeded bracket, the qualifying paths of the 2026-27 access list, no away goals, and UEFA coefficients simulated from the second season.

Italian football down to Serie D

The Serie B play-off and play-out with their real rules, Serie C in three groups and its 28-club play-off, the real Serie D 2026-27 in 9 groups with play-offs, play-outs and the Poule Scudetto, the Coppa Italia Serie D on its real bracket.

National cups as they really are

Every club still in its cup when the data was taken, from its real round, with the ties really drawn; league cups such as the EFL Cup and the Coppa Italia Serie C; no cup where none is played in 2026-27.

Real clubs, real squads

Squads at the end of the summer 2026 transfer window, fixed shirt numbers as today, real coaches and kits; the club you manage gets its 9 real reserves, 25 players in all.

Tables in colour, believable results

Every table shows its end-of-season zones, as on TV; simulated matches with real draw rates and scorelines; each club's strength checked against the real 2025-26 tables.

Already in the mod (1.0 – 3.3.6)

Kits

Purple kits where clubs wear them; a smarter automatic kit choice (black no longer clashes with blue, purple never meets maroon); a KITS button before every match opens SELECT KITS to change shirts, shorts and socks for that match.

Players

Shirt numbers from 17 to 99 and the numbers of the era in the historic packs; faces with dark skin and blond hair.

Italy 1996-97

Serie C1 and C2 with the real clubs, squads and coaches of the time, in career mode too: the end-of-season freeze of the old mods is fixed.

Club cups of every continent

Copa Libertadores (1997 exactly as it was), Supercopa and CONMEBOL, the Intercontinental Cup, the African and Asian club cups and the CONCACAF Champions Cup, all in career mode.

New countries and leagues

Costa Rica, Egypt, Morocco, Tunisia, Nigeria, Cameroon, Korea, China, Saudi Arabia, Guatemala, Honduras; Argentina with Apertura, Clausura and promedio; the Australian NSL Finals, New Zealand and the MLS 1997.

Historic worlds

The Europe 1984-85 career with the national teams of the era; classic seasons (DDR 1988-89, Serie A 1947-48) and classic tourneys (World Cups 1934 and 1982, Mitropa Cup 1934, FA Cup 1871-72).

Friendly to your game

Works with the CD and GOG versions and with SWOS++ (English exe); the patcher brings any earlier version up to date and never touches your saves.

How to install

Coming soon Version 4.0, with the World 2026-27, is coming soon: the patcher will be on this page on release day. These are the steps you will follow.

  1. Download swos-9697-mod-patcher.html from the release page and open it in your browser. It works offline too: nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  2. Drop in your language's executable and the game's DATA files, as the page asks, then download the patched files.
  3. Back up your game folder, copy the files into it (the TEAM files into DATA) and start a new career. In CLASSIC CAREERS choose WORLD 2026-27, then the continent and the country.

The patcher ships only differences and checks every file before and after patching: you need your own original copy of SWOS 96/97, CD or GOG. Already on an older version of the mod? Drop the files you have now: the patcher recognises them and brings them up to date. Saves are never touched.
