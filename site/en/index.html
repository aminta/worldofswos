What the mod is

SWOS 96/97 Mod patches your own copy of Sensible World of Soccer 96/97, the game we have played for thirty years, in all four of its languages: Italian, English, French and German.

Nothing of the game you love is lost: same pitch, same players running, same menus. What changes is the world around them. Version 4.0 brings a new career, World 2026-27: the 2026-27 season of 91 countries, every league, cup and play-off rebuilt exactly as its federation wrote it.

The highlights of 4.0

Every league in its real format

22 leagues split mid-season as their regulations say, promotion and relegation play-offs in 17 countries, European play-offs, Argentina's Apertura and Clausura with relegation by promedio, the MLS with its two conferences and the MLS Cup Playoffs.

European cups as UEFA plays them

The league phase of 36 with 8 matches (6 in the Conference League), one table with all ten UEFA tie-breakers, the knockout play-offs and the seeded bracket, the qualifying paths of the 2026-27 access list, no away goals, and UEFA coefficients simulated from the second season.

Italian football down to Serie D

The Serie B play-off and play-out with their real rules, Serie C in three groups and its 28-club play-off, the real Serie D 2026-27 in 9 groups with play-offs, play-outs and the Poule Scudetto, the Coppa Italia Serie D on its real bracket.

National cups as they really are

Every club still in its cup when the data was taken, from its real round, with the ties really drawn; league cups such as the EFL Cup and the Coppa Italia Serie C; no cup where none is played in 2026-27.

Real clubs, real squads

Squads at the end of the summer 2026 transfer window, fixed shirt numbers as today, real coaches and kits; the club you manage gets its 9 real reserves, 25 players in all.

Tables in colour, believable results

Every table shows its end-of-season zones, as on TV; simulated matches with real draw rates and scorelines; each club's strength checked against the real 2025-26 tables. Your club keeps its own colour in every table, never mixed up with the zones.

Who won what: the WINNERS page

A new page in the STAT menu lists who won each competition of the season: every Apertura and Clausura, every trophy and every play-off, at a glance. After the season end it also shows the previous season's winners, of the division your club played in (also after a promotion).

How to install

  1. Download swos-9697-mod-patcher.html and open it in Chrome or Edge (it works offline too).
  2. Click the button and choose the game folder (the one GOG launches: right-click "Launch Sensible World of Soccer 96-97" > Properties shows it). The patcher does the rest and keeps a backup. If the game is installed in the Program Files folder, the browser cannot modify it: copy the game folder to Documents, apply the patcher there, then copy it back to its place (Windows asks for administrator permission: accept). Alternatively use the manual mode (see the guide).
  3. Start the game: GOG's "Launch…" shortcut starts the English version (the green CAREER 2026-27 button means the mod is in). For Italian, French or German use a small launcher: see the guide. Do not double-click ITALIAN.EXE or ITALIAN.BAT: they are DOS programs and 64-bit Windows cannot run them; use GOG's shortcut or the launcher file below, which opens the game inside DOSBox.
  4. New career: in CLASSIC CAREERS choose WORLD 2026-27, or click the green CAREER 2026-27 button.

The patcher ships only differences and checks every file before and after patching: you need your own original copy of SWOS 96/97, CD or GOG. Already on an older version of the mod? Drop the files you have now: the patcher recognises them and brings them up to date. Saves are never touched.
