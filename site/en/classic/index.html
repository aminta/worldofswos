Classic careers, seasons and tourneys

Besides the World 2026-27, the mod holds whole historic worlds, single seasons and tournaments from football history, all in the game's own menus.

Europe 1984-85: a historic career

In CLASSIC CAREERS choose EUROPE 1984-85.

  • 32 European countries and 949 clubs of the 1984-85 season, with the real squads from DaniSwos's database.
  • Leagues in their 1983-84 order with promotion and relegation; the real 1984-85 national cups of 15 countries.
  • The European cups as they were: Champions' Cup 32, Cup Winners' Cup 32, UEFA Cup 64, with the real first-round ties and the away-goals rule of the time.
  • The national teams of the era (West and East Germany, the USSR, Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia: 143 teams) offer jobs: the real qualifying groups of the 1986 World Cup and Euro '88, Mexico '86 with 24 teams.
  • League tables in colour, with the zones of the time.

Classic seasons

One historic season to play, in CLASSIC SEASONS.

  • DDR 1988-89: the 14-club Oberliga (2 points a win), the FDGB-Pokal on its real bracket and, for four clubs, the real European cup of that season; real squads and coaches for all 155 clubs.
  • Serie A 1947-48: the Grande Torino's season, 21 clubs (one rests each round), 2 points a win, 4 relegated, real squads, coaches and kits; Torino's ratings from the SWOS 2020 1900s Retro Pack by FrancescoManetti82 and Gorzo.

Classic tourneys

In CLASSIC TOURNEYS, among the preset competitions.

  • World Cup 1934: 16 nations with their squads and coaches (from the SWOS 2020 DLC by Insane), the real bracket, replays, the third place match.
  • World Cup 1982 with its second round in 4 groups of 3, the Mitropa Cup 1934 and the first FA Cup, 1871-72, with byes and replays decided as back then.

Build your own

Seasons, worlds and tournaments are data, not code: anyone can prepare one and send it to us.

  • Season packs · Career worlds · Tourney packs (guides on GitHub, English and Italian).
