OpenSWOS-World-2627.zip -- the World 2026-27 for OpenSWOS
=========================================================

What it is: the World 2026-27 of the SWOS 96/97 Mod (github.com/aminta/swos-9697-mod), release 4.0.5,
converted for OpenSWOS (github.com/benbaker76/OpenSWOS) by its scripts/world_import.py. OpenSWOS Setup
downloads and installs it; the game checks its fingerprint (WORLD.ini) against the one baked into the build.
Contents: 91 countries' team files (JSON and the Amiga's TEAM.nnn), leagues.json, WORLD.ini.
Fingerprint: efe1464b6d1d62b8.

Data sources and credits
- Transfermarkt (transfermarkt.com): squads, market values (turned into SWOS prices), coaches, league
  members 2026-27.
- EA SPORTS FC ratings feed: player attributes (skill profiles).
- Wikipedia contributors (CC BY-SA 4.0): club kits; Wikidata (CC0): club <-> article links.
- Derived data only: no market values or ratings are included as such. The original SWOS 96/97 game data
  is not included.

World 2026-27 by Davide Lorigliola (SWOS 96/97 Mod); conversion by Ben Baker (OpenSWOS).
